Private Office Overview

The ZARIN Private Office serves buyers seeking premium Dubai real estate above AED 25 million. Frequently Asked

Private Office Questions

What is the ZARIN Private Office?

The Private Office is ZARIN's specialized service for buyers seeking premium Dubai real estate above AED 25M. It provides off-market access, multi-currency pricing, crypto facilitation, and full concierge services. Apply for access →

What is the minimum investment for Private Office services?

Private Office services are available for acquisitions starting from AED 25 million (approximately USD 6.8M). For acquisitions above AED 50M, enhanced concierge and wealth structuring services are included. Book consultation →

Can I pay for Dubai property with cryptocurrency?

Yes. Several Dubai developers accept cryptocurrency for property purchases. Each developer works with specific cryptocurrencies. ZARIN facilitates crypto payments through regulated exchange partners, subject to KYC compliance. Learn about crypto payments →

What currencies can I use to buy property in Dubai?

ZARIN's Private Office supports AED, USD, EUR, GBP, CHF, CNY, INR, and RUB. Live currency conversion is provided at the time of transaction. All payments are settled in AED through Dubai Land Department. View currencies →

What are off-market property listings?

Off-market listings are premium properties that are not publicly advertised. They are available exclusively through private networks. ZARIN's Private Office maintains access to 38+ off-market properties across Dubai's prime areas. Request off-market access →

What concierge services does the Private Office provide?

Concierge services include yacht and aviation partnerships, school search and relocation, staff recruitment, art and collectibles advisory, and lifestyle management. These are tailored to each client's specific needs. View concierge services →

How does ZARIN handle cross-border tax for international buyers?

ZARIN partners with cross-border tax specialists to help international buyers structure their Dubai acquisitions efficiently. This includes advice on tax residency, ownership structures, and double-taxation treaty optimization. View tax advisory →

How do I apply for Private Office access?

Submit a confidential inquiry through our website or contact us directly via phone or WhatsApp. After an initial consultation to understand your requirements, we provide curated access to off-market opportunities matching your mandate. Apply now →
