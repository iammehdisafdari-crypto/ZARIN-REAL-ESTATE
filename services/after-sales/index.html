After-Sales Support —
Custody Long After the Keys

The sixth stage of every ZARIN mandate. Utilities, tenancies, snagging claims, yields and renewals stay under advisory — one named advisor, long after the transfer appointment ends.

Handover starts ownership; it does not end the mandate. ZARIN keeps the file open. In the first thirty days we schedule the lot — DEWA and utility connections, Ejari tenancy registration where you are letting, building and contents insurance, and the snagging audit while the developer’s one-year defect liability window is still open. When the numbers argue for change, we rebalance — refinance, consolidate, or prepare the exit through our selling desk. The service is included with every completed ZARIN purchase; extended management is quoted per mandate, never as an invented percentage. Our founder has invested in Dubai real estate since 2005; the firm was established in 2023.

Stage Six · Custody

What After-Sales Support Is

Every ZARIN mandate runs to six stages: Understand Mandate, Unbiased Filtering, Due Diligence, Negotiate & Structure, Conveyancing & Closing — and then a sixth that never closes: Stay Supported (Custody). Conveyancing delivers the title deed; custody begins the moment the keys change hands. It is the difference between a transaction that ends at the trustee office and an asset that is actually looked after. On ZARIN purchases the handover is to our after-sales desk, not to a void.

Custody means advisor continuity and institutional memory. The advisor who negotiated your purchase knows your basis, your exit assumptions and the service-charge history you reviewed before committing — and keeps that file open. When a tenant query arrives in month fourteen, or the developer’s defect window is about to close in month eleven, the person handling it is not reading your file for the first time. That memory is the service: it is what turns a folder of documents into a managed position.

It is also deliberately different from ad-hoc property management. There is no call-centre rotation, no markup on contractor invoices, and no charge for picking up the phone. Work is scoped and evidenced in writing before it is done, an annual written review is built into the relationship rather than sold as an extra, and when the numbers argue for change we say so — including when the change is selling.

The Setup Calendar

The First 30 Days of Ownership

Six milestones, each scheduled before the transfer appointment rather than discovered afterwards. Off-plan buyers run the same calendar with Oqood registration in place of the title deed follow-up.

01

Title Deed & Registration Follow-Ups

The original title deed is collected and verified against DLD records, and any post-transfer registration items are closed out. On off-plan purchases, Oqood interim registration is confirmed so your interest is recorded from the first instalment. We chase the exceptions — mortgagor notings, developer NOC copies, parking and storage allocations — until the file is genuinely clean.

02

DEWA Connection & Utilities

Electricity and water are activated with DEWA, the security deposit is lodged, and the account is placed in the correct name — yours, or a tenant’s once Ejari is registered. Where the building runs district cooling, the provider account is opened in parallel so air-conditioning is live from day one. Moving-in permits and elevator bookings with the management company are arranged before your movers ask.

03

Ejari Registration If Letting

If the property is to be let, the tenancy contract is registered with Ejari — the legal requirement that underpins DEWA accounts in a tenant’s name, rent disputes and cheque enforcement. We prepare and lodge the registration, diarise the renewal, and set the notice calendar so rent revisions are never left to the final week of the term.

04

Insurance Placement

Building cover is placed — mandatory where a mortgage exists, and inexpensive enough to be standard practice regardless. Contents cover is quoted alongside for fitted and loose contents. Policies are sourced from UAE-licensed providers, with the schedule, excess and reinstatement basis explained in plain English before anything is signed.

05

Snagging Audit for New Builds

An independent snag survey is commissioned while the developer’s defect liability period — typically one year from handover — is still open. Every defect is documented, photographed and scheduled against the developer for rectification at their cost, not yours. Claims filed inside the window are materially stronger; claims filed after it are usually refused.

06

Access, Keys & Systems Handover

Access cards, fobs, parking permits and master keys are inventoried and held or couriered as you instruct. Service-charge accounts are opened with the owners’ association or management company, and the building’s rules — waste, deliveries, alterations, short-let policy — are summarised for you on one page. The property is now genuinely live.

The first month is the cheapest month

Defect claims, deposit corrections and account structures are all strongest when raised inside the first weeks. Every item on this calendar exists because leaving it a month later costs money, evidence, or both.

The Steady State

Ongoing Property Management

For owners who hand us the tenancy as well as the setup, management runs on the same principle as everything else ZARIN does: written evidence, one accountable advisor, and no markup anywhere in the supply chain. The four disciplines below are commissioned as a mandate — scoped and priced in writing before work begins.

Where the property is let, we also keep the rental market view current for your building — achieved rents, not asking rents — so renewal conversations happen on data rather than on a tenant’s hope or a landlord’s habit.

Tenant Sourcing & Vetting

Marketing across the major portals and our own applicant network, viewings conducted by the advisor who manages the file, and vetting that actually vettes: employment confirmation, credit standing and a prior-landlord reference before any offer is recommended. Contracts are RERA-compliant and registered with Ejari; deposits are held and documented to the dirham.

Rent Collection & Redeposit

Rent is collected on the due date, reconciled and redeposited to your account, with security deposits held and returned per the contract. Cheques that bounce are escalated the same week under Dubai tenancy law, not the same quarter. Where we manage collections, you receive a statement — not a chase.

Maintenance Coordination

Repairs are scoped in writing, quoted by vetted contractors, and invoiced to you at the contractor’s own price — ZARIN adds no markup. Work is inspected on completion for anything beyond routine items, and recurring issues are escalated to the owners’ association where they are structural rather than incidental.

RERA-Compliant Notice Handling

Rent revisions, non-renewals and eviction notices are drafted and served within the notice periods Dubai tenancy law requires — correctly dated, correctly served, correctly evidenced. Most disputes we see are lost on procedure, not principle; this is the discipline that prevents them.

When the numbers argue for an exit

Custody includes honesty about the sell decision. When the annual review says the position has run its course, we revalue the asset for free and hand you to our selling advisory — staging, refurbishment scoping and buyer screening included in that mandate.

Optional Service Line

Furnishing & Interior Coordination

This is coordination, not design. ZARIN manages the process end-to-end — developing the brief, shortlisting suppliers, designers and contractors, running the tender for quotes, holding the delivery timeline, and standing at the quality checkpoints at install — while the design talent and the execution stay with vetted specialists. You get one accountable coordinator for a supply chain that otherwise arrives as six separate contracts and a saga.

It matters most at three moments. Off-plan handovers, where fitted furniture and appliance packages must be locked in months before key collection to align with the developer’s handover date — miss that window and the unit sits empty while the millwork sits in a queue. Pre-let refreshes, in the weeks before a first tenancy or between tenancies, when paint, joinery and soft furnishing decide the achieved rent. And full furnishing programmes for owners who intend to let short-term, where furnishing quality directly drives the nightly rate and the occupancy.

The accounting discipline is the same one that governs our maintenance coordination. Quotes are obtained in the owner’s name wherever possible, so the price you see is the price the market quotes you. The no-markup rule applies without exception — ZARIN passes supplier and contractor invoices through at cost, because our fee is the coordination mandate, not a hidden margin on furniture. And every stage gate is approved by you in writing before the next one opens.

What This Is Not

ZARIN does not employ interior designers or run contracting crews. We coordinate, supervise, and account — you approve every spend before it is committed.

The creative work belongs to the designers and the trades you appoint from the tender; the responsibility for the process — timeline, quality and accounting — belongs to us.

01

Brief & Budget

The brief captures how the property will be used — owner-occupied, long-let, corporate or short-let — and sets a budget the tender must respect. Nothing is scoped before the purpose is fixed, because a holiday home and a family home are different furniture problems.

02

Shortlist & Tender

Suppliers, designers and contractors are shortlisted against the brief, then quoted in competition. Quotes are obtained in your name wherever possible and lined up side by side — scope, timeline and payment terms, not just price.

03

Delivery Timeline & Checkpoints

The winning tender is built into a delivery timeline anchored to your handover or letting date, with quality checkpoints at manufacture, delivery and install — so problems surface while they are still cheap to fix.

04

Snag, Sign-Off & Account

Install is snagged item by item and signed off in writing. The account closes with every supplier invoice passed through at cost and every approval on record — a schedule your future self can audit.

Income Strategy

Yield Optimisation — Long-Term vs Short-Term

Dubai’s citywide average runs at roughly 6-7% gross and AED 1,500 per square foot — but the area spread is wide, from Business Bay at 7.0% gross to Emirates Hills at 3.8%. How you let the property moves the number as much as where it sits. We model both routes against your actual service charges and your appetite for effort before recommending one.

There is no universally correct answer, and anyone who tells you otherwise is selling furniture. What follows is the honest version of the trade.

The Short-Term Route

Holiday-home letting requires a DET permit and, in most buildings, owners’ association approval before a single night is sold. Peak season — November to March — typically runs at 75-85% occupancy; summer is softer. It demands furniture, operations and utilities at your cost, and it can outperform long-term letting on total return when it is run properly.

The Long-Term Route

Long-term letting trades headline rent for stability. Furnished stock commands a spread over unfurnished in the corporate-let segments of Downtown Dubai and Dubai Marina; the size of the spread depends on area and tenant profile. On payment terms, a single cheque buys a premium over multiple cheques; multi-cheque terms buy cash-flow security. We present the trade — you pick the side.

The Written Review

Annual Portfolio Review & Rebalancing

Once a year, in writing, we sit down with the whole picture: what each asset has actually returned against its area benchmark, what the refinancing window looks like, and whether the portfolio’s shape still matches the mandate. The founder has run his own Dubai portfolio this way since 2005; the firm was established in 2023 to give clients the same discipline.

Refinance windows are reviewed against live terms — non-resident lending at 50-65% loan-to-value on EIBOR-linked rates of roughly 3.5-5.5%, with Emirates NBD, Mashreq and ADCB the most active desks. Where equity has built and the rate argues for it, we structure the refinance; where it does not, we say nothing and save you the arrangement fee.

The same review asks the structural question: consolidate or diversify? Selling two studios to buy one larger asset in a stronger micro-location reduces administration and void risk; adding a second asset spreads it. Both are legitimate — the answer changes with your decade, not your postcode. Where values cross the AED 2M threshold, the review also flags the evidence a Golden Visa filing requires.

And where a portfolio needs structuring beyond property — ownership, succession, cross-border matters — the file escalates to our Private Office rather than hitting a wall.

After-Sales FAQ

Eight Questions Owners Ask First

Utilities, snagging windows, tenancies, management costs and the exit — answered with process rather than adjectives. If your question is not here, ask us directly.

What happens after I receive the keys?
Ownership begins; the mandate continues. Within the first thirty days ZARIN’s after-sales desk schedules the entire setup — title deed follow-ups, DEWA and district-cooling connections, Ejari registration if you are letting, insurance placement, and the snagging audit while the developer’s one-year defect window is open. From month two the file settles into custody: tenancy or holiday-home operations where commissioned, maintenance with vetted contractors, and a written annual review against area benchmarks. The core setup and review are included with every completed ZARIN purchase; extended management is quoted per mandate. You keep one advisor — the one who already knows why you bought.

Can ZARIN set up DEWA and utilities?
Yes — it is milestone two of the setup calendar. We activate the DEWA account, lodge the security deposit, and place the account in the correct name: yours for a home you occupy, or a tenant’s once an Ejari-registered tenancy exists. Where the building runs district cooling, the provider account is registered in parallel, and moving-in permits and elevator bookings are arranged with the management company before furniture arrives. We need surprisingly little from you — identification, the title deed and a forwarding email — and everything else is handled by the desk that already holds your file. Activations typically complete within days of the transfer, not weeks.

What is snagging and when must it happen?
A snagging audit is an independent, item-by-item defect survey of a newly handed-over property — paint, tiling, joinery, sanitaryware, doors, electrics, air-conditioning, and the less visible items such as waterproofing. On Dubai new builds, the developer’s contractual defect liability typically runs one year from handover. Defects documented and filed inside that window are rectified at the developer’s cost; claims filed after it closes are routinely refused. ZARIN commissions the survey early, schedules it against the developer, and chases rectification to closure — which is precisely why the audit sits in the first thirty days rather than in the first argument with a contractor.

Can ZARIN find and vet tenants?
Yes — sourcing is available as part of a management mandate. Marketing runs across the major portals and our own applicant network, and viewings are conducted by the advisor who will manage the tenancy, not a stranger with a keypad. Vetting is the part most owners skip at their cost: employment confirmation, credit standing and a reference from the prior landlord are obtained before we recommend accepting an offer. Contracts are RERA-compliant, registered with Ejari, and deposits are held and documented to the dirham. For furnished and corporate lets we can also structure mid-term terms that sit between the two letting strategies.

How much does ongoing management cost?
Extended management — full tenant management, holiday-home operations, refurbishment project management — is quoted per mandate, in writing, before any work begins. The figure depends on scope: a single long-term tenancy is a different proposition from a furnished holiday home running peak-season operations, and we price them differently rather than inventing a percentage. Two things are constant. First, the 30-day setup, snagging coordination and annual written review are already included with every completed ZARIN purchase. Second, we add no markup to contractor invoices — maintenance is billed at the contractor’s own price, always evidenced, never rounded.

Can you manage my property if I live abroad?
Most of our custody files are owned from abroad, and the desk is built for it. Inspections run on video with the advisor walking the property; documents that need signing are executed under a notarised power of attorney; and communications work to your time zone rather than Gulf Standard Time’s. DEWA and service-charge accounts are structured so nothing requires your physical presence, rent is reconciled and redeposited wherever you bank, and spend above an agreed threshold is approved by you before it is committed. Overseas owners typically hear from us more, not less — because distance is no reason to be less informed.

How often will I hear from ZARIN?
On a rhythm, not on a whim. The first thirty days run to a written setup calendar with a milestone report as each item completes. Where we manage the tenancy, you receive regular rent and maintenance statements. Annually, you receive the written portfolio review — performance against area benchmarks, the refinance window, and a hold/sell analysis. And immediately, you hear about anything material: a bounced cheque, a leak, a developer letter. What you will not get is a newsletter pretending to be advice, or silence pretending to be calm. The founder has applied that reporting discipline to his own portfolio since 2005; the firm was established in 2023 to offer it to clients.

Can after-sales help me sell later?
Yes — and custody is what makes the exit efficient. Because we hold the file — your basis, the snag history, the tenancy terms, the service-charge ledger — the revaluation takes days rather than weeks, and it is free. When the review says the position has run its course, we revalue the asset, scope any refurbishment that would repay itself at sale, and hand you to selling advisory for staging, marketing and buyer screening. Tenanted properties are sold with the tenancy disclosed properly and vacant possession priced honestly — so neither you nor the buyer discovers a surprise at transfer.
