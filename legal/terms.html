5. Fees & Commissions

Our fee structure is transparent and disclosed before any engagement. The typical structure is:

  • ·Buyer-side commission: 2% of property purchase price, payable at title transfer. This is the standard UAE RERA-aligned commission.
  • ·Seller-side commission: 2% of sale price, payable at title transfer.
  • ·Tenancy agreements: 5% of annual rent for residential, 7% for commercial, payable by tenant per UAE market practice.
  • ·Mortgage & visa coordination: included at no extra charge for clients transacting through ZARIN; standalone services are quoted separately.
  • ·Initial consultation: free, no obligation.

All commissions are subject to 5% UAE VAT. We issue tax-compliant invoices for every transaction. No hidden fees, no surprise charges — if a fee applies, we disclose it before you commit.

7. Transactions & Contractual Relationships

When you proceed to a property transaction through ZARIN, separate contractual relationships arise between you and the seller, developer, mortgage lender, escrow agent, and other parties. ZARIN acts as your advisor and broker; we are not a party to the underlying sales contract.

For each transaction, we will guide you through Form A, Form B, Form F, Form I, and Form U as required by RERA. We coordinate with the DLD for Oqood (off-plan) and Title Deed registration. All payments — including deposits, installments, and final balances — are made directly to the developer's escrow account or the seller's designated account, never to ZARIN.

Our advisory fee is invoiced separately and payable upon successful title transfer. If a transaction does not complete for reasons beyond your control (e.g., developer default), no commission is due.
