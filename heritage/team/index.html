Structure Is Strategy

Why a Small Senior Team

Most Dubai brokerages are built to move volume: dozens of agents, each incentivised to close, none incentivised to stay. The client gets handed off — lead to closer to processor to a service desk that has never heard of you. ZARIN was founded in 2023 as the structural opposite. Four senior people, each capable of running a complete mandate alone, each accountable for the files they run.

The rule that follows is simple: one advisor, end-to-end. The person who takes your brief conducts the viewings, challenges the developer’s numbers, negotiates the structure, sits at the transfer — and answers the phone in month fourteen when the tenancy needs attention. Nothing about your file is ever explained twice, because it is never handed over once.

Small also means honest about capacity. We take the mandates we can run properly and decline the ones we cannot — the same discipline the founder applies to his own portfolio since 2005. A team that cannot say ‘not this month’ is a team that has already said ‘not properly’.

Two Models, One Table
Volume brokerage
A rotating cast of agents and juniors; your file is re-learned at every handoff, and the incentive is the commission, not the outcome.
ZARIN senior team
One named advisor who already holds the whole file — brief, shortlist, negotiation history — and answers for it, in writing, every year.
The arithmetic
Four senior advisors running complete mandates serve more buyers properly than forty agents running fragments. Quality scales by refusal, not recruitment.

The Process

One Advisor, Six Stages

Every ZARIN mandate — every desk, every nationality — runs the same six stages, and the same named senior advisor runs them all. The methodology is documented in full on the buying advisory page; the point of this page is who carries it.

01

Understand Mandate

Budget, financing position, holding period, visa intent and family circumstances — the brief is written before a single listing is opened.

02

Unbiased Filtering

The whole market screened against the brief, including projects we decline to sell. Only what fits survives to a shortlist.

03

Due Diligence

Developer record, escrow status, service charges, comparables, title verification — the numbers behind the brochure.

04

Negotiate & Structure

Price, payment terms, holding structure and mortgage or remittance sequencing — negotiated by the advisor who will close the file.

05

Conveyancing & Closing

MOU to transfer appointment: DLD fee, NOC, trustee office, title deed — roughly 30 days on a standard file.

06

Stay Supported

After-sales custody: DEWA and Ejari setup, snagging windows, tenancy and the annual written review. The stage that never closes.

Stage six is where teams are tested

Most brokerages are organised to reach the transfer. ZARIN is organised around what follows it — the after-sales custody your advisor keeps after the keys change hands. It is the stage that makes ‘one advisor, end-to-end’ a promise rather than a slogan.

Team FAQ

Eight Questions Buyers Ask About the Team

Team size, advisor continuity, languages, licensing and how the desk system works — answered plainly. If your question is not here, ask the team directly.

How big is the ZARIN team?
Four senior people: the founder plus three senior advisors — Rohit Kapoor on the India desk, Emma Whitfield on the UK desk and Li Hong on the China desk. The size is deliberate, not a constraint. ZARIN was founded in 2023 as a boutique advisory, and every mandate is run by someone senior enough to run it alone. When a file needs specialist execution — mortgage, legal, insurance — we coordinate vetted partner firms rather than padding the payroll.

Will I work with the same advisor throughout?
Yes — that is the design. One named advisor runs all six stages of your mandate: Understand Mandate, Unbiased Filtering, Due Diligence, Negotiate & Structure, Conveyancing & Closing, and Stay Supported. The same person who learned your brief on day one handles your DEWA query in month nine, because they hold the file’s institutional memory. Teams rotate; advisors here do not.

Can I choose which advisor or desk handles my file?
In practice the brief chooses for you: an Indian buyer with FEMA questions belongs with the India desk, a UK buyer weighing non-dom structuring with the UK desk. But you can request an advisor, and if your file is complex the founder attends personally. What you cannot get is a junior — there are none. Start on the consultation page and the routing takes care of itself.

What languages does the team work in?
The firm’s working languages are English, Arabic and Farsi — EN · AR · FA — across correspondence, contracts and Ejari paperwork. The country desks add native-market fluency on top: the India desk reads FEMA circulars like a Mumbai accountant, the UK desk speaks HMRC’s language, and the China desk handles SAFE documentation in its original form. The advice arrives in whichever of the three working languages you prefer.

How do I verify ZARIN's licence?
ZARIN Real Estate is a RERA-licensed brokerage operating under Dubai Land Department regulation, and the founder is a RERA-certified advisor. You do not have to take our word for it: the licence can be verified on the DLD website or through the official Dubai REST app. We will walk you through the lookup on a first call — a brokerage that resents being verified is telling you something.

Does the founder still advise clients directly?
Yes, selectively. He reviews every file at the filtering and due diligence stages — that is where his two project-level investments and his investing since 2005 change outcomes — and he attends consultations on complex portfolios and off-plan project questions. Day-to-day execution belongs to the senior advisors, because a founder who micro-runs every file has not built a team. Read his letter for how he splits the two roles.

What happens if my advisor is travelling or unavailable?
The file pauses; the service does not. Every ZARIN mandate is documented to a standard any senior colleague can pick up — brief, shortlist, due diligence notes, negotiation history — and a small senior team means cover comes from people who run the same process, not from a stranger reading your file for the first time. Time zones are worked to yours, not ours. Urgent items reach the desk same-day, holidays included.

How are advisors compensated?
One way: the 2% buyer-paid commission on completed purchases, disclosed in writing before you sign. Advisors earn no developer incentives, no listing bonuses, no volume kickbacks — so an advisor’s only incentive is the outcome you keep. That single line explains most of what is different about this team, and it is the founder’s rule from his own years on the buyer’s side of the table.
