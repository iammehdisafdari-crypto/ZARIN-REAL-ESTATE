Frequently Asked

Questions About Dubai Properties

What types of properties are available for sale in Dubai?

Dubai offers apartments, villas, penthouses, townhouses, and branded residences. ZARIN's portfolio includes trophy properties in Palm Jumeirah, Downtown Dubai, Dubai Hills, and Business Bay. Read buyer's guide →

What is the average price of luxury property in Dubai?

Luxury properties in Dubai range from AED 5M ($1.4M) for high-end apartments to AED 50M+ ($13.6M) for trophy villas and penthouses. Prime areas like Palm Jumeirah average AED 2,400 per sqft. Use cost calculator →

Which areas in Dubai are best for property investment?

Top investment areas include Palm Jumeirah (5.5% yield), Dubai Marina (6.8%), Downtown Dubai (6.2%), and Business Bay (7.5%). Each offers different yield-to-entry ratios. Compare all areas →

Can foreigners buy freehold property in Dubai?

Yes. Since the 2002 Freehold Decree, foreigners can own freehold property in designated Dubai areas. Title deeds are issued by the Dubai Land Department (DLD). Read non-resident guide →

What are branded residences in Dubai?

Branded residences are luxury properties developed in partnership with premium brands like Bugatti, Armani, Cavalli, Bentley, Mercedes-Benz, and Aston Martin. They offer brand-managed services and design. View branded residences →

What is the difference between off-plan and ready property in Dubai?

Off-plan properties are bought directly from developers before construction completes, often with payment plans. Ready properties are completed and available for immediate move-in. Off-plan typically costs 15-22% less. View off-plan projects →

What is the minimum investment for a Dubai Golden Visa?

The 10-year Golden Visa requires a minimum property investment of AED 2 million (USD 545,000), verified by the Dubai Land Department. Check eligibility →

Can I view Dubai properties virtually before visiting?

Yes. ZARIN offers 360-degree virtual tours and video viewings for most properties. We also provide detailed floor plans, payment schedules, and ROI analysis before any in-person visit. Request virtual viewing →
