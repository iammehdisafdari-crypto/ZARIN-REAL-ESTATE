Area Overview

Dubai's prime areas range from AED 1,400/sqft in Business Bay to AED 2,400/sqft on Palm Jumeirah. Gross rental yields span 5.5% to 7.5%, with Business Bay offering the highest yields and Palm Jumeirah delivering the strongest capital appreciation. Each area serves a different buyer profile — from waterfront luxury to golf community family living.

Frequently Asked

Questions About Dubai Areas

Which Dubai area is best for property investment in 2026?

Business Bay offers the highest yield at 7.5%, while Palm Jumeirah provides the strongest capital appreciation. For balanced returns, Dubai Marina (6.8%) and Downtown Dubai (6.2%) are top choices. View ranking →

What is the most expensive area to buy property in Dubai?

Palm Jumeirah is the most expensive at AED 2,400 per sqft, followed by Downtown Dubai at AED 2,100. Emirates Hills and Jumeirah Bay Island also command premium prices for trophy villas. View Palm Jumeirah guide →

Which Dubai areas allow foreigners to buy freehold property?

Foreigners can buy freehold property in all designated freehold areas including Palm Jumeirah, Downtown, Dubai Marina, Dubai Hills, and Business Bay. These were established by the 2002 Freehold Decree. Read non-resident guide →

What is the cheapest area to buy property in Dubai?

Business Bay (AED 1,400/sqft) and Dubai Hills Estate (AED 1,500/sqft) offer the most accessible entry points among prime areas. JVC and Dubai South are even more affordable but less established. View Business Bay guide →

Which Dubai areas have the best rental yields?

Business Bay leads with 7.5% gross yield, followed by Dubai Marina (6.8%), Downtown Dubai (6.2%), and Dubai Creek Harbour (6.0%). Palm Jumeirah yields 5.5% but offers stronger capital appreciation. View comparison →

Is Dubai Creek Harbour a good investment?

Yes. Dubai Creek Harbour is Emaar's flagship future development, planned around the new Creek Tower. Current prices average AED 1,900/sqft with 6.0% yield, expected to rise significantly as infrastructure completes. View Creek Harbour guide →

Which Dubai area is best for families?

Dubai Hills Estate is the top family community, offering villas, golf courses, schools (including GEMS and Repton), parks, and Dubai Hills Mall. Arabian Ranches and JVC are also popular family choices. View Dubai Hills guide →

How does Dubai property compare to London or Singapore?

Dubai offers higher rental yields (6-8% vs London's 3-4% or Singapore's 2-3%), no property tax, and Golden Visa eligibility. However, London and Singapore offer more established legal frameworks. View Dubai vs London →
