Area Overview

Dubai Hills Estate at a Glance

Dubai Hills Estate is a joint venture between Emaar Properties and Meraas — an 11 sq km masterplan launched in 2013 between Al Khail Road and Sheikh Mohammed Bin Zayed Road, strategically positioned between Downtown Dubai and Dubai Marina. The plan allocates the majority of its land to open space: an 18-hole championship golf course, the 2.5 million sqft Dubai Hills Mall (opened 2022), and Dubai Hills Park anchor the centre, while more than 10,500 homes — apartments, townhouses, and villas — are planned across sub-communities such as Maple, Sidra, Acacia, and Golf Place. Construction continues through 2027, though the mall, golf club, park, schools, and Metro Route 2020 station are already operational, giving buyers a mature community today with completion upside still to come.

The estate has positioned itself as Dubai's flagship family community — the default choice for executive families trading up from Marina or Downtown apartments. The resident base skews toward owner-occupiers: parents who chose the estate for GEMS International School and Delhi Private School a few minutes away, golf members who walk to the first tee, and professionals who value the ten-minute drive to Downtown offices. Weekends revolve around the mall's dining terrace, park runs, and junior sports academies. The streetscape is deliberately low-density — villa enclaves are gated, apartment clusters top out at mid-rise, and walkways connect neighbourhoods to the park and golf clubhouse. It is quieter, greener, and more child-centric than any of Dubai's other established premium districts.

The investment case rests on three numbers: 11.2% five-year price CAGR — the fastest of Dubai's established areas; 5.5% gross rental yields backed by family tenants who stay multi-year; and service charges of AED 12 per sqft per year, the lowest among premium districts, which protects net returns. Supply is the structural support: the masterplan completes through 2027, and remaining releases are increasingly premium — signature villas now trade at AED 12M-35M — while entry apartments from AED 950K keep the market deep. Emaar's escrow-backed delivery record and the 2022 mall opening have already repriced the estate upward; ZARIN's view is that the remaining completion phases add infrastructure rather than competing supply.

How to read these numbers

Dubai Hills Estate prices segment cleanly by product. Apartments transact around AED 1,650 per sqft, townhouses around AED 1,450, and villas around AED 1,750 — the villa premium reflects fairway frontage and plot scarcity. Entry pricing starts at AED 950K for a one-bedroom in earlier releases, with newer Emaar launches quoting AED 1.3M-1.9M; townhouses begin at AED 2.8M, villas at AED 4.5M, and signature golf-frontage villas at AED 12M-35M. Five-year appreciation has been strongest exactly where land is scarcest: villas (+78%) have outpaced townhouses (+74%) and apartments (+68%). For buyers, the practical implication is that golf-view and park-front product carries both the highest entry price and the most durable resale liquidity.

Lifestyle & Amenities

Life in Dubai Hills Estate

The four amenities that shape daily life — and property values — in the district.

Golf Course & Club

The estate's centrepiece is an 18-hole championship course designed by European Golf Design — a par-71 layout with downtown skyline views from the elevated holes. The clubhouse anchors the community socially: residents' dining, the academy, and year-round junior programmes. Ownership does not include membership, but resident-tier rates and priority tee times apply. Fairway-adjacent addresses carry the estate's clearest premium, and the course doubles as permanent green belt that no future phase can build on.

Dubai Hills Mall

The 2.5 million sqft Dubai Hills Mall, opened in 2022, put a full regional retail centre inside the community — hypermarket, fashion, a cinema multiplex, and a dining terrace that functions as the estate's living room. For families the practical effect is weekly life without leaving the masterplan; for investors it was the single biggest repricing event in the estate's history, lifting values across every sub-community within walking distance.

Schools & Family

GEMS International School sits inside the estate and Delhi Private School is minutes away, with nurseries and early-years centres distributed through the masterplan. The resident base is dominated by families — weekends belong to park runs, mall terraces, and sports academies, and the street network is designed around gated villa enclaves and walkable clusters. School-run logistics, more than golf, are what drive this community's property demand — ZARIN checks current year-group waitlists before purchase.

Parks & Green Space

Dubai Hills Park is the estate's central green — open lawns, sports courts, playgrounds, and running tracks connecting the mall, schools, and residential clusters. Combined with the golf course's permanent green belt and the landscaped boulevards, roughly half the masterplan is open space. For buyers, that density cap is the product: views stay green, traffic stays internal, and park-facing addresses hold a persistent premium over identical interior stock.

Transport in Dubai Hills Estate

Dubai Hills Estate sits between Al Khail Road and Umm Suqeim Street, with Sheikh Zayed Road access at the southern boundary — the triangle that makes both Downtown (10 minutes) and Dubai Marina (15 minutes) easy commutes. The Route 2020 Metro extension's Dubai Hills Estate station, on the community's western edge, connects to the Red Line and puts DXB Airport 15 minutes away by car. GEMS International School and Delhi Private School are both within a five-minute drive, and the estate's internal road network keeps school-run and mall traffic off the residential streets — a detail that shows up in how quietly the villa enclaves actually live.

Capital Appreciation 2020–2026

Dubai Hills Estate entered 2020 around AED 970 per sqft — a young community still trading at a discount to its own replacement cost. The 2021-2022 villa boom lifted golf communities first: estate villas appreciated more than 60% in eighteen months as family demand outstripped released plots. The February 2022 opening of Dubai Hills Mall repriced the whole district, and Route 2020's full operation removed the last commute objection. From 2023 through 2026 the market normalised into steady 8-12% annual gains, bringing the five-year CAGR to 11.2% — the fastest of any established Dubai area — with apartments at AED 1,650, townhouses at AED 1,450, and villas at AED 1,750 per sqft today.

Outlook 2026–2028

2026-2028 looks constructive but maturing. Remaining launches complete through 2027 and skew premium — the marginal buyer is a villa and signature-villa buyer, not an apartment investor — which supports the top of the market while earlier apartment releases keep the entry band liquid. ZARIN's base case is high-single-digit annual price growth with yields holding near 5.5%, helped by the AED 12 per sqft service-charge advantage. The variables to watch: the pace of the final villa releases, school waitlist dynamics (which gate family inflow), and the relative pull of newer golf communities on the same buyer profile.

Dubai Hills Estate FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Eight questions ZARIN advisors hear most often from Dubai Hills Estate buyers. If yours isn't covered, message us on WhatsApp for a direct answer — typically within an hour during business hours.

What is the average price per sqft in Dubai Hills Estate in 2026?
Apartments average AED 1,650 per sqft, townhouses AED 1,450, and villas AED 1,750. Entry pricing starts at AED 950K for a one-bedroom apartment in earlier releases (newer Emaar launches quote AED 1.3M-1.9M), two-beds from AED 1.6M, three-beds from AED 2.8M, townhouses from AED 2.8M, and villas from AED 4.5M. Signature golf-frontage villas trade at AED 12M-35M. Fairway and park frontage carries a 15-25% premium over interior product of the same specification.

Is Dubai Hills Estate a good investment?
Over five years the estate has compounded prices at 11.2% annually — the fastest of Dubai's established areas — while paying 5.5% gross rental yields. Service charges of AED 12 per sqft per year are the lowest among premium districts, which lifts net yield relative to Dubai Marina (AED 18) or Business Bay (AED 20). The masterplan completes through 2027 with remaining releases skewed premium, and the mall, golf course, park, and Metro station are already operational. ZARIN's base case is continued high-single-digit growth with stable yields.

Is Dubai Hills Estate family-friendly?
It is Dubai's flagship family community. GEMS International School sits inside the estate and Delhi Private School is minutes away; the 18-hole golf course, Dubai Hills Park, and the mall's family floors anchor weekends; and the street network is designed around gated villa enclaves and walkable apartment clusters rather than through-traffic. The resident base is dominated by owner-occupier families, which shows up in the data: longer tenancies, lower churn, and steadier resale demand than in investor-heavy districts.

What are the service charges in Dubai Hills Estate?
Approximately AED 12 per sqft per year — the lowest of Dubai's premium districts. On a 2,000 sqft townhouse that is roughly AED 24,000 annually, and on a 5,000 sqft villa about AED 60,000, covering security, landscaping, common-area maintenance, and community management. Exact charges vary by sub-community, building vintage, and management arrangement; ZARIN verifies the current charge schedule for each specific listing before purchase so net yields are modelled on the real number.

Can foreigners buy freehold property in Dubai Hills Estate?
Yes. Dubai Hills Estate is a designated freehold area — foreign nationals of any nationality can buy apartments, townhouses, and villas with outright freehold title registered with the Dubai Land Department. No UAE residency visa is required to purchase, and the property can be inherited, mortgaged, or resold without restriction. Purchases above AED 2M also qualify for the 10-year Golden Visa, which most Dubai Hills villas and many townhouse transactions exceed. ZARIN handles the full transfer: KYC, NOC, escrow, and DLD registration.

Which schools serve Dubai Hills Estate?
GEMS International School – Dubai Hills Estate operates inside the community itself, and Delhi Private School is a short drive away, both drawing significant enrolment from estate families. Numerous nurseries and early-years centres operate within the masterplan, and the Al Khail corridor puts further KHDA-rated schools within 15 minutes. Demand for places is high — ZARIN advises buyers with school-age children to check current waitlists for their specific year groups before committing, as school placement is the estate's most common relocation friction point.

Does buying in Dubai Hills Estate include golf membership?
No — ownership and membership are separate. The 18-hole championship course is operated by Dubai Hills Golf Club, and residents receive preferred rates and priority tee-time windows rather than automatic membership. Membership categories and resident discounts change seasonally; current joining terms are available from the club directly, and ZARIN confirms the latest resident pricing as part of every golf-view mandate. Many estate residents never join, valuing the course instead for its green outlook and the low-density buffer it creates around villa enclaves.

When will Dubai Hills Estate be fully complete?
The masterplan launched in 2013 and completes through 2027. Core infrastructure is already delivered — Dubai Hills Mall (2022), the golf course and clubhouse, Dubai Hills Park, schools, and the Route 2020 Metro station — with the remaining phases concentrated on later villa and apartment releases. Practically, this means buyers today get a functioning community with completion upside: each handover adds residents and retail rather than uncertainty, and Emaar's escrow-backed delivery structure protects off-plan buyers through the final phases.
