The Short Version

ZARIN publishes seven free calculators covering the full acquisition maths of a Dubai purchase: mortgage repayments, rental ROI net of service charges, off-plan payment schedules, RERA-indexed service charges, currency conversion across eight currencies, a seven-question visa eligibility check, and an AI match brief that returns a curated shortlist from our advisory desk. Every tool runs in your browser — no signup, no personal data collected — on lookup tables compiled from DLD transaction data and the RERA service-charge index and reviewed quarterly. A bonus eighth output, the Instant Indicative Market Estimate, arrives with the free Market Price Assessment. Our founder has run this arithmetic on his own portfolio since 2005; the firm was established in 2023 so that every buyer could run it too — before the dirhams move, not after.

The Eighth Output

Bonus: the Instant Indicative Market Estimate

Not a calculator in the classic sense but the output buyers ask for most: what is this property worth, right now, roughly? The instant estimate is part of the free Market Price Assessment — enter an address or an area and size, and the tool returns an indicative range around the area benchmark within seconds.

The example on the right is the canonical one: a 1,000 sqft Dubai Marina apartment benchmarks at AED 1,850 per sqft, giving a range of roughly AED 1,702,000 to AED 1,998,000 with an indicative rent of about AED 118,400 — a 6.4% gross yield at the midpoint. Specific floor, view, condition and tenancy move a real unit within — and occasionally outside — that range.

Where the assessment earns its keep is what follows the instant number: a Selling Price Recommendation, a Rental Price Assessment, a Current Market Competition Analysis, a Property Selling Strategy and an Off-Plan Resale Assessment — five deliverables, written, free.

Under the Hood

How the Math Works

All seven calculators run entirely in your browser. There is no signup, no account, no email gate — and no personal data collected: the numbers you enter never leave your device. What powers the results are lookup tables compiled from DLD transaction data and the RERA service-charge index, reviewed every quarter and shipped with the page itself.

The formulas are deliberately transparent — no black boxes, no “proprietary algorithms” guarding an arithmetic you could do on paper. If a tool ever produces a number you cannot reconstruct from the inputs, that is a bug, and we want to hear about it.

Monthly repayment

The mortgage tool amortises the loan (price minus down payment) over the term at the rate you enter — the standard annuity formula, the same one your bank uses.

Gross yield

Annual rent divided by purchase price. Simple by design: a Marina unit at AED 1,850,000 renting near AED 118,400 is 6.4% gross.

Net yield

(Annual rent minus service charges) divided by purchase price. The ROI Predictor pulls the service-charge line from the RERA index before showing you the net figure.

Buyer’s total costs

DLD transfer fee 4% + ZARIN commission 2% + admin AED 4,580 — plus mortgage registration at 0.25% of the loan when financed.

Why browser-based matters: nothing transmitted means nothing to leak, nothing to sell and nothing to subpoena. It also means the tools work on a plane, in a viewing, or on a building’s wifi — the three places buyers actually run numbers.

Provenance

Data Sources & Cadence

Every figure the calculators produce traces back to two public sources: DLD transaction data — the registry of what actually sold, for what, recorded by the Dubai Land Department — and the RERA service-charge index, the regulatory benchmark for annual ownership costs by building and district.

The cadence has two gears. The underlying DLD transaction records refresh every 24 hours on the market data dashboard. The lookup tables inside the calculators — area prices, yields, service-charge bands — are reviewed quarterly, on the ZARIN 2026 ROI basis, so a released table is always a considered snapshot rather than yesterday’s spike.

Nothing is changed silently. Each quarterly review is logged in the changelog inside the off-plan investment guide — which line moved, why, and what it does to a worked example. The same discipline our founder has applied to his own files since 2005; the firm was established in 2023 to make it standard practice.

Calculators FAQ

Eight Questions About the Tools

Cost, privacy, accuracy and cadence — answered with the same transparency the calculators themselves run on. If your question is not here, ask us directly.

Are all seven calculators really free?
Yes — all seven tools are free, with no signup, no email gate and no payment wall. They exist because informed buyers make better clients: our founder has run exactly this arithmetic on his own Dubai portfolio since 2005, and the firm was established in 2023 to give every buyer the same numbers before a dirham is committed. The only thing we ask in return is that you talk to us when the numbers argue for a purchase.

Do you collect the numbers I enter?
No. Every calculator runs entirely in your browser — the inputs never leave your device, nothing is transmitted to our servers, and no personal data is collected. There is no account to create and nothing to delete afterwards. The lookup tables behind the results (area prices, yields and the RERA service-charge index) live in the page itself, refreshed with each quarterly review.

Which calculator should a first-time buyer start with?
Start with the mortgage calculator to convert a price into a monthly figure, then read the buying-costs worked example on the buying advisory page: an AED 2,000,000 cash purchase costs AED 124,580 in total acquisition costs (6.2%), or AED 132,830 (6.6%) with a 60% loan-to-value mortgage. Knowing the monthly number and the all-in cost is the whole first conversation.

How accurate is the Instant Market Estimate?
It returns an indicative range around the area benchmark — for a 1,000 sqft Dubai Marina apartment, roughly AED 1,702,000 to AED 1,998,000 around a AED 1,850,000 midpoint. It is an advisory indicative market appraisal for guidance only: it is not a formal valuation and does not replace a bank or DLD valuation. When you need a property-specific number, the free Market Price Assessment adds a five-part written report.

How often are the lookup tables updated?
Quarterly. The tables compile DLD transaction data and the RERA service-charge index, and each review is logged in the changelog inside the off-plan investment guide. The underlying DLD transaction records refresh every 24 hours on the market data dashboard, so the quarterly table is always a reviewed snapshot of live data, not a stale one.

Can non-residents use the mortgage calculator?
Yes — the inputs reflect how Dubai actually lends to overseas buyers: non-resident mortgages typically run at 50–60% loan-to-value through partner banks, with income documented from abroad. Model your monthly figure with that ceiling in mind, and note that the DLD 4% transfer fee, 2% commission and AED 4,580 admin fee apply identically whether you pay cash or finance. For live terms, the desk coordinates introductions to the banks active in non-resident lending.

What is the difference between gross and net yield in the ROI Predictor?
Gross yield divides annual rent by purchase price; net yield subtracts service charges first. On a 1,000 sqft Dubai Marina unit at AED 1,850,000 renting for about AED 118,400, the gross yield is 6.4% — but about AED 16,000 of service charges takes the net figure to roughly AED 102,400, or 5.5% net. The ROI Predictor shows both lines, because quoting only the gross number is how disappointment is manufactured.

Do the tools work on my phone?
Yes. Every calculator is built for the browser — phone, tablet or desktop — with no app to download and no plugin to install. Results recalculate instantly as inputs change, so you can sanity-check a payment plan or a currency conversion during a viewing, standing in the property the numbers are about. The pages are lightweight by design; the maths is simple and the interface stays out of its way.
