Our Process

Six Stages of Measured Acquisition

Every ZARIN mandate follows the same institutional process — from mandate definition through long-term custody.

01

Understand Mandate

Comprehensive mandate definition — investment thesis, risk parameters, lifestyle requirements, exit strategy, and timeline.

02

Unbiased Filtering

From the full Dubai market inventory, we filter to a shortlist of 5-8 properties that match your mandate.

03

Due Diligence

Independent valuation, developer reputation check, escrow health verification, service charge analysis, and legal review.

04

Negotiate & Structure

Price negotiation, payment plan structuring, SPA review, and deal architecture optimized for your tax position.

05

Conveyancing & Closing

DLD transfer, NOC acquisition, title deed registration, utility activation, and key handover — managed end-to-end.

06

Stay Supported (Custody)

Property management, tenant sourcing, annual yield optimization, portfolio rebalancing, and disposition strategy.

Fee Transparency

All Costs Disclosed Upfront

We believe in full transparency. Every cost associated with your Dubai property purchase is disclosed before you sign — no hidden fees, no surprises at closing.

Agency commission: 2% of purchase price (buyer pays in Dubai)
DLD transfer fee: 4% of purchase price
Title deed fee: AED 4,000
Mortgage registration: 2% (if financed)
Annual service charges: AED 10-30 per sqft (varies by area)

Frequently Asked

Services Questions

What services does ZARIN Real Estate offer in Dubai?

ZARIN offers eight core services: buying advisory, selling advisory, property management, valuation, mortgage advisory, legal and conveyancing, property insurance, and after-sales support. View all services →

How much does ZARIN charge for real estate services?

Standard agency commission is 2% of the property purchase price, paid by the buyer per Dubai market convention. Valuation, mortgage advisory, and legal services may have separate fee structures. All fees are disclosed upfront. View fee breakdown →

Can ZARIN help me get a mortgage as a non-resident?

Yes. ZARIN's mortgage advisory connects non-resident buyers with UAE banks (Emirates NBD, Mashreq, ADCB) offering up to 50-65% LTV at 3.5-5.5% variable rates linked to EIBOR. Learn about mortgages →

Does ZARIN provide property management services?

Yes. ZARIN offers full property management including tenant sourcing, rent collection, maintenance coordination, and annual yield optimization. Available for both long-term and holiday rental properties. View property management →

How does the buying process work with ZARIN?

The process follows six stages: understand mandate, unbiased filtering, due diligence, negotiation, conveyancing and closing, and after-sales support. ZARIN manages the entire process end-to-end. View process →

Can ZARIN help me sell my Dubai property?

Yes. ZARIN's selling advisory includes free property valuation, marketing strategy, buyer screening, negotiation, and DLD transfer management. We also advise on optimal timing based on market conditions. Learn about selling →

Does ZARIN handle legal and conveyancing?

Yes. ZARIN's in-house legal team handles SPA review, NOC acquisition, DLD title deed transfer, and utility activation. All legal work is conducted through RERA-compliant channels. View legal services →

What is included in after-sales support?

After-sales support includes property management setup, utility connection, tenant sourcing, annual yield optimization, and portfolio rebalancing. We remain your advisor long after the keys are handed over. View after-sales →
