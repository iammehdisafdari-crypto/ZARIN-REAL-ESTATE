Why Buy at New Launch

Four Advantages of Phase 1

Buying at new launch stage is materially different from buying later phases. Here's what makes it strategically valuable.

Lowest Entry Price

Phase 1 pricing is typically 10-15% below Phase 2, and 20-30% below handover pricing. The early-bird discount reflects the developer's need to demonstrate sales velocity to banks and investors.

Best Payment Plans

Developers offer the most attractive payment plans at launch — extended 60/40, 70/30, or even 50/50 structures over 5+ years. Later phases typically shorten payment windows and reduce flexibility.

Priority Unit Selection

First-in buyers choose the best units — penthouses, marina-facing apartments, corner villas with private pools. By Phase 2, the prime inventory is typically gone. ZARIN secures priority booking slots for clients.

Capital Appreciation Window

Most off-plan projects appreciate 15-30% between launch and handover. Phase 1 buyers capture this appreciation from the lowest base — multiplying returns on capital deployed.

Launch FAQ

Common Launch Questions

What is a new launch in Dubai off-plan property?

+
A new launch is the very first sales phase of an off-plan project, when the developer releases the first batch of units to the market. New launches typically offer the lowest entry prices of the entire project lifecycle, alongside the most attractive payment plans. Early buyers benefit from capital appreciation as later phases release at higher prices. Phase 1 inventory is also where the best unit selections (penthouse, corner, premium views) are available.

How do I register interest for a new launch?

+
Registration typically requires KYC documents (passport copy, proof of funds) and an Expression of Interest (EOI) form. ZARIN handles this for clients — we submit your interest to the developer, secure priority booking slots, and accompany you to the launch event. Some high-demand launches use a balloting system where all registered buyers have an equal chance at unit selection. Pre-registration is critical for any premium launch.

What is the typical payment plan for new launches in 2026?

+
Most new launches in 2026 offer 60/40 payment plans (60% during construction, 40% on handover) over 3-5 years. Some projects offer 70/30 or 50/50 plans. Premium projects like Emaar Beachfront and Palm Jebel Ali use 70/30. Off-plan mortgage is available on handover. ZARIN reviews every payment plan with you before commitment, including installment timing tied to construction milestones.

Are new launches cheaper than ready properties?

+
Generally yes. New launches typically price 15-25% below comparable ready properties, reflecting construction risk and the wait for handover. The trade-off is opportunity cost of capital tied up during construction. ZARIN analyzes each launch's pricing against ready comparables to verify the discount is genuine — some launches are priced close to ready and don't offer meaningful Phase 1 advantage.

What is the risk of buying at new launch stage?

+
Main risks are: developer delay or default, project cancellation, and construction quality issues. Mitigations include: buying from reputable developers (Emaar, Nakheel, Meraas, DAMAC have strong track records), checking escrow account status, reviewing construction bond if applicable, and ensuring RERA registration. ZARIN's developer due diligence covers all of these before any client commits to a new launch.

Can I sell my off-plan unit before handover?

+
Yes — this is called 'flipping' and is permitted by most developers with their approval. You typically need to have paid 30-40% of the unit price and obtain an NOC from the developer. Transfer fees apply (4% DLD + agency). Profit potential exists if the project has appreciated since launch — but flipping carries risk if the market softens or the developer restricts pre-handover transfers.

Do I need a mortgage pre-approval before buying off-plan?

+
Not at the booking stage — most off-plan purchases only require the booking deposit (10-20% of price). However, if you plan to mortgage the balance at handover, getting pre-approval before signing makes sense. Some developers offer in-house mortgage arrangements. ZARIN coordinates with major UAE banks on your behalf to plan your financing strategy from day one.

What documents are required to register interest in a new launch?

+
Typically: passport copy, proof of funds (bank statement or letter), and a signed Expression of Interest form. For UAE residents: Emirates ID copy as well. Corporate buyers need: trade license, MOA, board resolution. ZARIN prepares the EOI paperwork and submits to the developer on your behalf, ensuring all documents meet the developer's specific requirements.

How quickly do new launch units sell out?

+
Premium launches from established developers (Emaar, Nakheel, Meraas) can sell out the first phase within hours of release. Mid-tier projects typically sell out over 1-4 weeks. Registration of interest before the launch date is critical for high-demand projects — ZARIN secures priority booking slots for registered clients.
