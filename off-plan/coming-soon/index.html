Four Reasons to Register Before Launch

Pre-launch registration is the single highest-leverage move a Dubai off-plan buyer can make. The window is short and the inventory is small — here is what registering early actually buys you.

Pre-Launch Pricing

Developers reward early commitment with pricing 5 to 10 percent below the public launch figure. The discount reflects real value — it is the price of demonstrating traction to lenders and partners before public marketing begins. ZARIN verifies each pre-launch price against comparable ready and off-plan transactions so you know the discount is genuine.

Priority Unit Selection

Penthouses, marina-facing apartments, corner villas with private pools, and prime floor levels are reserved for pre-registered buyers. By the time the public launch opens, these units are typically allocated or already booked. ZARIN secures priority booking slots so you receive the full inventory sheet 24 to 72 hours before the public release.

First to Know

Registered clients receive launch announcements and pricing revisions 24 to 72 hours before the public release, alongside project renderings, draft payment plans, and indicative handover dates. You will also receive ZARIN's pre-launch briefing memo summarizing the opportunity against comparable off-plan and ready transactions in the same community.

Early-Bird Payment Terms

Pre-launch payment plans often feature lower booking deposits, longer interest-free installments, and extended handover windows compared to the public launch version. Some developers add a 1 to 2 percent additional discount on the down payment for pre-launch buyers. ZARIN reviews the full payment schedule against your financing plan before you commit.

Pre-Launch FAQ

Common Pre-Launch Questions

What is a pre-launch in Dubai real estate?
A pre-launch is the window before a developer officially releases a project to the public. During this period a select group of pre-registered buyers receives confidential project details, indicative pricing, and the first opportunity to reserve units. Pre-launch access is typically limited to vetted investors and broker partners, and the entire window usually lasts 2 to 12 weeks. ZARIN secures pre-launch invitations for registered clients across all major Dubai developers.

How is pre-launch different from new launch?
A new launch is the official first day of public sales, when any buyer can register interest and book a unit. Pre-launch is the period 2 to 12 weeks before that date, when pricing and inventory are shared only with a closed circle of pre-vetted buyers. Pre-launch prices are typically 5 to 10 percent below the new launch price, and pre-launch buyers also access the best unit lines — penthouses, premium views, and corner villas — before they are released to the broader market. The trade-off is that pre-launch requires more paperwork up front, including KYC and occasionally an NDA.

Can anyone register for pre-launch access?
Most developers restrict pre-launch to investors who have completed KYC and proof of funds in advance. Some confidential projects require a signed non-disclosure agreement before any project details are shared. ZARIN runs the KYC and eligibility check on your behalf and matches you to projects that fit your budget, area, and unit-type preferences. Registration is free, carries no obligation to purchase, and your details are never shared with the developer until you explicitly authorize it.

What information do I get during pre-launch?
Pre-launch briefings include project renderings, the master plan, unit type and floor plate layouts, indicative pricing, the draft payment plan, expected handover date, and the developer's escrow account and RERA registration details. Some projects release the full sales brochure only at the public launch, but pre-launch buyers see an indicative version 24 to 72 hours earlier. ZARIN summarizes every briefing into a single decision memo so you can compare projects side by side, alongside comparable ready and off-plan transactions in the same community.

Is pre-launch pricing really lower?
Yes, in most cases. Developers price pre-launch 5 to 10 percent below the public launch price to reward early commitment and to demonstrate sales traction to lenders before public marketing begins. The discount reflects the buyer's willingness to commit before public marketing begins, and it is genuinely off the launch price — not a sticker reduction ahead of a higher headline. ZARIN verifies each pre-launch price against comparable ready and off-plan transactions to confirm the discount is real, and we flag any project where the discount looks nominal.

What happens if I change my mind after registering?
Registration is an expression of interest, not a purchase commitment. You can withdraw at any time before signing the booking form and paying the deposit, with no fee and no penalty. ZARIN notifies you at each gate — KYC, NDA, briefing, booking slot — so you can step back gracefully at any stage, even after receiving the full briefing pack. Your personal data is retained per our privacy policy and used only for related launch communications; you can request deletion at any time by emailing advisory@zarinrealestate.com.

How early before launch can I register?
Most pre-launch windows open 4 to 12 weeks before the public launch date, though some confidential projects accept registrations up to 6 months in advance. The earlier you register, the wider your selection of units and the more time you have to arrange financing, KYC, and any required NDAs. ZARIN recommends registering interest at least 8 weeks before the expected launch date for high-demand projects — this gives you time to complete verification and receive the full briefing pack without rushing the decision.

Do all developers offer pre-launch access?
No. Pre-launch access is offered by established developers with strong broker relationships, including Emaar, Nakheel, Meraas, DAMAC, Sobha, and Ellington. Smaller or first-time developers typically launch directly to the public because they do not yet have a broker network vetted to handle confidential pricing. ZARIN maintains pre-launch agreements with all major Dubai developers and updates the project pipeline weekly as new pre-launch slots are confirmed. If a developer you are interested in is not currently offering pre-launch, we will register you for the public launch and notify you the moment sales go live.
