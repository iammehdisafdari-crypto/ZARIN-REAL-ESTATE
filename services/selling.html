Selling Advisory —
Exit With Evidence, Not Hope

Sell-side counsel that prices on evidence, screens every buyer, and manages the DLD transfer through to funds cleared — with no upfront fees.

The Short Version

ZARIN's selling advisory is a disciplined exit. It begins with a free, DLD-based market price assessment that prices your property against recorded comparables rather than optimism, and sets an asking band with a defensible floor. Marketing then climbs a three-tier ladder — private network, agent-to-agent, controlled public launch — and escalates only with your written approval. Every buyer is screened before an offer is tabled: proof of funds or a bank mortgage pre-approval, verified, not assumed. Negotiation and the DLD transfer are managed end-to-end — NOC or mortgage discharge, service-charge settlement, DEWA clearance, trustee appointment — with title transfer typically around 30 days from agreement. The seller pays no upfront fees: the assessment, photography and marketing are carried by ZARIN, and the 2% commission is paid by the buyer per Dubai market convention on ZARIN mandates. Our founder has invested in Dubai real estate since 2005; the firm was formally established in 2023.

Sell-Side Counsel

What Selling Advisory Includes

Selling advisory is full-lifecycle sell-side counsel: from the decision to exit, through the market price assessment, pricing, marketing, buyer screening and negotiation, to the DLD trustee appointment and the final utility clearance. One named advisor owns your file throughout — the person who priced the property is the person who negotiates the offer and sits at the transfer. Nothing is handed off to a junior, and nothing is left to the buyer's side to organise on your behalf.

Advisory is not a listing service. A listing service photographs the apartment and waits for the phone to ring. Counsel means timing advice — whether to exit into the current delivery pipeline or hold through it; pricing strategy — an asking band anchored to recorded DLD transactions with a defensible floor; presentation decisions — when professional staging or minor works will return their cost and when they will not; and negotiation — structured offers, screened counterparties, and completion discipline that survives last-minute pressure.

The service fits the whole exit spectrum: apartments and villas, tenanted or vacant, mortgaged or unencumbered, off-plan resales near handover, and incomplete off-plan assignments where the position itself — not a deed — is what changes hands. Whatever the asset, the sequence is the same: evidence first, price second, reach third, and paperwork that never stalls for want of a document you were never told about.

Step One

Market Price Assessment & Pricing Strategy

Every mandate begins the same way: a written market price assessment delivered within two working days of receiving your title details, priced against what has actually closed — not what neighbours are asking. The assessment is free, carries no obligation, and arrives as an asking band with a reserve floor, so the pricing conversation starts from evidence on both ends.

Four inputs build it. Together they replace the two numbers most sellers are quoted — the optimistic one and the insulting one — with a range you can defend at the trustee office.

Request the Free Assessment
Full service detail on the Market Price Assessment page — DLD comparables, delivered in 48 hours

DLD Transaction Comparables

We price from recorded Dubai Land Department transactions in your building and district — what actually closed, not what is being asked. Portal prices are marketing; DLD records are evidence.

Three-Year Service Charge History

A building whose service charges have climbed 40% in three years prices differently from a stable one. The ledger is part of the assessment, because buyers' advisors will read it.

View, Floor & Condition Adjustments

Like-for-like adjustments for the factors that move price per square foot: orientation, floor plate, refurbishment state, and the difference between staged photography and the snag list.

Band Pricing, Not a Single Number

You receive an asking band with a reserve floor — the range where evidence says the asset trades. Pricing is a strategy, not a wish: the band is set to generate competition without leaving money on the table at the first credible offer.

Reach, In Escalating Tiers

Marketing Your Property

Marketing climbs a three-tier ladder. You start at the tier that matches your priorities — discretion, calibrated reach, or maximum exposure — and escalate only when the evidence says so. Every escalation is approved by you in writing before a single new party sees the listing.

Tier 1

Private Network

Your property is introduced discreetly to vetted premium buyers within ZARIN's own network and Private Office registry. The address is generalised to neighbourhood level until a buyer is pre-qualified; no public listing exists at any point.

When to choose it

Choose when privacy matters more than reach — high-value assets, sensitive ownership, or simply a quiet exit.

Tier 2

Agent-to-Agent

The listing is shared selectively with partner brokerages under non-disclosure agreement, expanding reach into their buyer books while controlling who holds your information and on what terms.

When to choose it

Choose when Tier 1 has not produced a qualified offer within the agreed window and reach must widen without going public.

Tier 3

Controlled Public Launch

Full portal launch — Property Finder and Bayut — with professional photography, floor plans, and a video walkthrough produced to a brief you approve. Maximum reach, with every enquiry still screened before a viewing.

When to choose it

Choose when the asset competes on price and presentation, and reach is the priority you have signed off.

Discretion-led sells

For high-value assets where the exit itself is private information, the ladder runs entirely inside our Private Office — NDA-bound counterparties, generalised address, and no public footprint at any tier.

Only Serious Buyers

Buyer Screening & Negotiation

A viewing costs you nothing; a false buyer costs you a month. So no offer proceeds at ZARIN without proof of funds or a verified mortgage pre-approval — one hundred percent of the buyers we table are screened, and the discipline shows up in completion rates rather than in headline enquiry counts.

Once an offer is credible, negotiation is structured rather than theatrical: the deposit, the payment schedule and the completion conditions are drafted to protect your position, and pressure from rival bidders is handled transparently — never manufactured, never used to re-trade a deal you have already signed.

Proof of Funds, Verified

Cash buyers produce a bank letter or verified statement, current within weeks — not a screenshot and not a promise. Funds that cannot be evidenced do not earn a viewing.

Mortgage Pre-Approval, Confirmed

Financed buyers present a lender's pre-approval letter, checked for amount, validity and property type. We know which banks are actually closing at what LTV this quarter.

Offer Structuring

Deposit percentage, payment schedule, completion conditions, and chattels are drafted into the agreement — not improvised at the trustee office. Every clause is explained before you sign it.

Pressure, Managed Ethically

Dubai has no binding auction culture, but re-trading pressure exists. We handle it with short offer validity, clear terms, and backup buyers held transparently — never used to blackmail a signed deal.

Escrow & Trustee Protection

Deposits and completion monies move through escrow or the DLD trustee office, never through personal accounts. Your exposure to a defaulting counterparty is structurally limited.

Completion

Transfer & Handover Management

The last mile of a Dubai sale is administrative, and it is where undisciplined sales stall: a NOC that arrives late, a discharge letter the bank needs ten working days to issue, service charges that were never quite current. ZARIN runs the sequence below as a managed checklist, so the trustee appointment completes rather than adjourns — and the money moves the same day.

01

NOC or Mortgage Discharge

The developer issues the no-objection certificate (fee typically AED 500-5,000, service charges required current); where a mortgage exists, the bank's discharge letter is sequenced into the timeline.

02

DLD Trustee Appointment

The transfer is booked at the Dubai Land Department trustee office; funds, paperwork and both parties are staged so the appointment completes rather than adjourns.

03

Service Charge Settlement

Outstanding service charges are settled to date before the NOC is issued — a precondition, not a surprise at the table.

04

DEWA Clearance

The electricity account is closed out and cleared; the buyer registers afresh. Settlement only — there is no fee to the seller for the clearance itself.

05

Tenancy Handover (If Occupied)

Where the property is tenanted, the Ejari contract, deposit and rent ledger transfer to the buyer under the existing terms, with notice properly served and documented.

Timing Counsel

When to Sell — Reading the 2026 Market

Two forces set the timing question in 2026. The first is the delivery pipeline: a substantial volume of new handovers concentrated in a handful of districts, which caps capital growth exactly where supply lands. The second is rental growth, the engine of the repricing cycle — where rents keep rising, values follow, because the buyer of today underwrites on today's income. An exit decision is a judgement about which force wins in your building's district, not a general feeling about Dubai.

The evidence sits in the per-square-foot data: citywide, Dubai averages around AED 1,500 per square foot with 6-7% gross yields. Downtown Dubai trades at AED 2,200 per square foot yielding 5.8-6.2%; Palm Jumeirah at AED 2,800 yielding 4.9%; Dubai Marina at AED 1,850 yielding 6.4%; Business Bay at AED 1,750 yielding 6.8-7.5%; Dubai Hills at AED 1,650 yielding 5.5%; and Creek Harbour at AED 2,100 yielding 5.9%. High-yield districts carry more of their return in income; prime districts carry it in capital. The exit question differs accordingly.

The honest answer is that no one times the top of a cycle — they price the trade-off. Our founder has invested in Dubai real estate since 2005, through the 2008 correction and the long plateau that followed; the firm was formally established in 2023 to sell exactly that pattern recognition rather than optimism. Where the property anchors a Golden Visa, the exit also needs a replacement plan — see our Golden Visa guidance before you list.

Hold vs Sell — The Arithmetic

A worked example on a AED 2,000,000 apartment at a 6% gross yield, 1,300 square feet carrying AED 15/sqft in service charges, held over 5 years. The seller's own exit costs are small; the real friction of leaving the market is re-entering it, because the buyer's side pays the DLD fee and the commission on the way back in.

Read plainly: 5 years of holding generate roughly AED 502,500 of net rental income — about 25% of the property's value — while selling and re-entering at similar value consumes about AED 124,580, or roughly 15 months of net income. Unless the alternative use of your capital is expected to beat a ~25% five-year carry by a clear margin, holding usually wins; where a genuine opportunity exists, the round trip is affordable and we will say so.

Selling FAQ

Eight Questions Sellers Ask First

Costs, timelines, commission, mortgages, tenants, assignments and remote signing — answered with the numbers attached. If your question is not here, ask us directly.

How much does it cost to sell with ZARIN?
Nothing upfront, and — on standard ZARIN mandates — nothing at completion either. The market price assessment, photography, floor plans, portal listing fees and viewings are carried by ZARIN, and the 2% agency commission is paid by the buyer per Dubai market convention. The seller's genuine cash costs are modest and largely administrative: a developer NOC fee of AED 500-5,000, a mortgage discharge fee of AED 0-1,000 if a loan is being settled, service charges brought current, and DEWA clearance, which is a settlement rather than a fee. On a typical AED 2M unencumbered sale, seller-side out-of-pocket costs come to roughly AED 3,000 — about 0.15% of the sale price. Every line is quoted in writing before the mandate is signed.

How long does a Dubai sale take?
From mandate to money, typically six to ten weeks. The first fortnight is pricing and preparation: the DLD-comparable market price assessment, photography, floor plans, and the marketing ladder you approve in writing. Tier 1 and Tier 2 marketing often produce screened buyers within two to four weeks on correctly priced stock; a controlled public launch widens the funnel if needed. Once an offer is accepted, the mechanics are fixed: the developer NOC (five to ten working days, sometimes longer), mortgage discharge if applicable, and the DLD trustee appointment — with title transfer and funds released the same day. Around 30 days from signed agreement to transfer is the working assumption we plan to.

Do I pay the agent commission?
No — on ZARIN mandates the buyer pays. Dubai market convention assigns the 2% agency commission to the buyer's side of the transaction, and ZARIN's mandates are structured accordingly: the seller pays no commission at completion. This is worth stating plainly because it is the reverse of the UK and much of Europe, where sellers absorb the fee. It also means our sell-side counsel has no incentive to discount your price to hurry a completion — we are not financing the sale out of your proceeds. Where a counterparty or cooperating brokerage structures matters differently, the fee position is disclosed in writing before any offer is tabled, so there are no surprises at the trustee office.

What documents do I need to sell?
The core pack is short: passport copy (and Emirates ID if you hold one), the title deed, and — for mortgaged property — the bank's payoff letter. If the property is tenanted, the current Ejari-registered tenancy contract and rent ledger; if off-plan, the original SPA and payment history. Non-resident sellers signing remotely add a notarised power of attorney. During the process, the developer issues the NOC — which requires service charges to be current — DEWA is cleared at handover, and the DLD trustee office holds the balance of the paperwork. We issue the checklist for your specific property at mandate sign-up; most sellers gather everything in under a week.

Can I sell with an outstanding mortgage?
Yes — most Dubai resales settle an existing mortgage as part of completion, and it is routine rather than exceptional. The buyer's funds, or their mortgage proceeds, are applied at the trustee office; your bank issues a discharge letter once its payoff figure is met; the mortgage is released and title transfers to the buyer in the same appointment chain. Your costs are a discharge fee of roughly AED 0-1,000 depending on the bank, plus timing discipline — banks typically need ten working days' notice on the discharge letter, which we build into the schedule. Where the property is worth less than the loan, the shortfall must be funded separately: a conversation worth having before marketing, not after an offer.

Can I sell a tenant-occupied property?
Yes — and the tenancy transfers with the property: the new owner inherits the tenant, the rent, and the Ejari contract on its existing terms. RERA's rules protect the tenant's continuity, and notice periods and rent caps apply to the landlord relationship, not to a sale. Practically, a well-documented tenancy with a clean payment history is an asset rather than a liability — it sells the property as a performing investment with income from day one, particularly to yield-focused buyers. We market tenanted stock with the rent ledger in the data room and coordinate viewing access on proper notice. If your goal is vacant possession, the eviction timeline must be planned before marketing begins, not negotiated after an offer arrives.

Can I sell an incomplete off-plan property?
Yes — through an assignment, transferring your rights and obligations under the original SPA to a new buyer. Developer consent is required, developers typically charge a NOC or assignment fee of AED 500-5,000, and your payment plan must be current. The buyer steps into your schedule; you realise the premium the market has built, if it has moved your way. What ZARIN adds is honest pricing of the position: assignments compete with the developer's own remaining inventory, so the achievable premium depends on how much of the tower is left, how attractive the payment plan is, and the delivery timeline. We model that before you commit to a price — optimism is not a strategy in the assignment market.

Can I sign everything remotely with power of attorney?
Yes. A notarised power of attorney — drafted to cover signing the sale agreement, executing the DLD transfer, and receiving sale proceeds — lets the entire disposition complete without you in the country. A POA issued outside the UAE must be attested for local use; one issued inside needs only a notary appointment. Our trusted legal partners manage the attestation chain, and the POA can be revoked the moment funds are confirmed. The same trilingual coverage — English, Arabic and Farsi — applies on the sell side, which matters when developer NOC correspondence runs in Arabic. The founder has completed Dubai transactions this way since 2005; the firm, formally established in 2023, was built to make remote exits routine.
