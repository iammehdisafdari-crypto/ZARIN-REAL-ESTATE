The Short Version

ZARIN Real Estate is featured in four established outlets — Forbes Middle East, Arabian Business, Property Finder and the Gulf Real Estate Excellence Award — and provides commentary to Gulf business media on Dubai’s residential market. This page deliberately avoids fabricated clippings: no invented headlines, dates or bylines. What we offer instead is precise: the themes we comment on — market data (Q3 2026: AED 1,650 per square foot citywide, +12% year on year, 38,247 transactions, roughly 6.5% average gross yield), off-plan payment structures, the AED 2M Golden Visa pathway and area dynamics across the eight areas we cover — and the rules we comment by: data over predictions, “we don’t know” when we don’t, and no developer endorsements. The media kit, key facts and a 24-hour press contact are below. The founder has been active in Dubai real estate since 2005; the firm was founded in 2023.

Commentary Themes

What We Comment On

ZARIN comments on Dubai’s residential market for business media. The four themes below are the ones we are asked about, and the ones we will speak to on the record — with the numbers attached. Everything else gets an honest “not our desk”.

Market Data

The quarterly picture, DLD-sourced: in Q3 2026 the citywide average stood at AED 1,650 per square foot, up 12% year on year, with 38,247 recorded transactions and an average gross yield of roughly 6.5%. We comment on what the data says — including when it says “wait”.

Off-Plan Payment Structures

How staged payment plans, escrow protection and Oqood registration actually work — and what a payment schedule implies about total cost. We plan these with the off-plan payment calculator and speak about them in plain terms.

The Golden Visa Pathway

The AED 2M minimum property threshold, the 10-year term, full family sponsorship, and the rule that mortgaged property qualifies only if the unpaid balance is no more than 50% of the title deed value. Visa commentary is where rumour does most damage; we stick to the rules on the Golden Visa page.

Where the Numbers Come From

Dubai Land Department records, tracked daily and published quarterly. Our market reports carry the full analysis — the Q3 2026 edition runs 42 pages — and the market data pages update between editions.

When a figure is an estimate rather than a record, we say so on the record. That distinction is the difference between commentary and noise.

House Rules

Our Commenting Rules

Three rules govern every comment ZARIN gives the press. They exist because commentary is a service to readers, not marketing with a byline — and because a firm that disciplines its own numbers since 2005 should discipline its quotes the same way.

Data Over Predictions

We comment on what has happened and what the rules say — transaction volumes, recorded prices, published visa criteria. We do not forecast prices on the record, because nobody reputable can. Where a journalist needs a view on the future, we give scenarios with their assumptions attached.

We Say “We Don’t Know”

When we don’t know, we say so — on the record. A tight comment (“I don’t have that figure; here is who records it”) serves a story better than a confident guess. It is also why journalists come back.

No Developer Endorsements

We do not endorse developers or projects in the press. We advise on developers inside mandates — the developer reputation tracker exists for that — but “buy this developer” is not a sentence ZARIN says on the record.

Commentary is a service, not marketing. Nothing we say to a journalist is for sale, and nothing is said to move a listing. If a quote cannot be defended with a number or a published rule, it does not leave the building.

Formats

Interview Formats We Accept

Three formats, all workable within a normal news cycle. What we do not accept: ambush quotes, invented quotes, or “just a quick line on whether now is the time to buy” — that one gets the honest answer every time.

01

Written Q&A

Send questions by email; we return answers with the data sources attached, typically within 24 hours. Written answers keep numbers exact — no transcription risk between our desk and your page. Attributable unless you agree otherwise in advance.

02

Phone or Video Briefing

A 15–30 minute briefing with the ZARIN Founder or the relevant senior advisor — desk-matched to the story: India, UK, China or Iran/GCC focus. Recordings welcome. We speak English, Arabic and Farsi, so the briefing runs in whichever language the story needs.

03

Background Briefing

Non-attributable context on market conditions — useful when a journalist needs to understand a mechanism (escrow, Oqood, visa rules) without quoting anyone. We ask only that facts taken on background are checked before publication.

Press & Media FAQ

Eight Questions Journalists Ask First

Interviews, data, endorsements and republication — answered the way we answer on the record. If your question is not here, the press desk is 24 hours away.

How do I request an interview or a comment?
Email advisory@zarinrealestate.com or use the contact page — both reach the desk directly. We respond within 24 hours, usually faster; if your deadline is tighter than that, say so in the subject line. Tell us the outlet, the angle and the format you want — written Q&A, phone or video briefing, or background.

What topics can ZARIN comment on?
Four themes, in depth: market data (Q3 2026: AED 1,650 per square foot citywide, up 12% year on year, 38,247 transactions, roughly 6.5% average gross yield), off-plan payment structures, the AED 2M Golden Visa pathway, and area dynamics across the eight areas we cover. Deeper material sits in our market reports. Outside those themes we will say so rather than improvise.

Will ZARIN endorse a specific developer or project?
No. ZARIN does not endorse developers or projects in the press — no exceptions, and no paid version of an exception. We advise on developer standing inside mandates, where the developer reputation tracker does that job with evidence. On the record, our commentary stays at the market level.

Does ZARIN have a media kit?
Yes — it is on this page. The boilerplate “About ZARIN Real Estate” (about 60 words), the logo usage standards (gold-on-ink, dark backgrounds) and the key facts box are cleared for publication. For anything else — specific imagery or data tables — email the press contact and we will send what we can properly stand behind.

Can ZARIN provide data for my story?
Yes. The quarterly numbers we quote are DLD-sourced — the Q3 2026 snapshot and the eight-area benchmark table above are the standard set — and our market data pages update daily. We share figures with their sources attached, and we flag anything that is an estimate rather than a record. What we do not do is dress a guess up as a statistic.

Which languages can ZARIN comment in?
English, Arabic and Farsi, written and spoken — the same three languages the advisory works in. Written Q&A can be returned in any of the three; briefings can be conducted in whichever the journalist prefers. Desk-matched senior advisors handle India, UK, China and Iran/GCC-focused stories.

Where has ZARIN been featured?
In four established outlets: Forbes Middle East, Arabian Business, Property Finder — whose Best Brokerage recognition ZARIN holds for 2025 — and the Gulf Real Estate Excellence Award. This page deliberately cites no article titles, dates, URLs or bylines: we do not fabricate clippings, and we will not pad the list. If you need to verify a specific claim about our coverage, ask and we will point you to the outlet.

Can I republish ZARIN data or charts?
With attribution: credit ZARIN Real Estate and, where relevant, Dubai Land Department as the underlying source. Our market reports and market data pages are the canonical versions — republish from there rather than from a screenshot. Our AI disclosure explains where AI assists our published analysis, for transparency.
