What is the current state of Dubai real estate in 2026?

Dubai real estate in 2026 continues strong growth with average prices up 12% year-on-year. Prime areas like Palm Jumeirah average AED 2,400/sqft, while Business Bay offers 7.5% gross yields. View market data →

Where can I find Dubai real estate market reports?

ZARIN publishes quarterly market reports with DLD transaction data, price trends, and yield analysis. Download free reports from our market reports hub. Download reports →

What is the average ROI on Dubai real estate in 2026?

Average gross yields range 5-9%. Business Bay leads at 7.5%, Dubai Marina at 6.8%, Downtown Dubai at 6.2%, and Palm Jumeirah at 5.5% with stronger capital appreciation. Calculate your ROI →
