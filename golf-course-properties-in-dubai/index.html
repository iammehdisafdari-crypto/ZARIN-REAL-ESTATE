Golf Communities

Five Fairway Frontiers

Dubai's golf property market is concentrated across five communities, each anchored by a championship golf course designed by a globally recognised architect. Some communities offer direct fairway frontage on your title deed; others provide elevated views across the course from a townhouse balcony. The choice shapes both lifestyle and investment case — an Emirates Hills mansion on the Majlis Course behaves very differently from a Jumeirah Golf Estates townhouse on the Earth Course. ZARIN advisors have walked every community and can speak to view corridors, plot orientations, and recent resale trends in each.

Why Golf Properties

The Case for Golf Course Property

Dubai's golf property is a finite, asymmetric asset class. Only 5% of the city's residential supply sits within a championship golf community, and almost no new golf masterplan land has been released since Tilal Al Ghaf. That scarcity — combined with lifestyle, capital appreciation, and rental premium dynamics — makes golf view property one of the strongest sub-segments of Dubai residential for the next 5-7 years. Below are the four reasons ZARIN mandates golf course property for clients seeking both lifestyle and capital preservation.

Reason 01

Scarcity

Dubai has 9 championship golf courses, of which only 5 are surrounded by freehold residential development. Emirates Hills, Dubai Hills Estate, Jumeirah Golf Estates, Arabian Ranches, and Tilal Al Ghaf account for the entire fairway-frontage villa and townhouse supply. No new golf masterplans have been released since Tilal Al Ghaf launched in 2018 — golf community land is finite by definition.

5% of Dubai supply
Reason 02

Lifestyle

Golf community living in Dubai is not symbolic — it means an unobstructed fairway view from your living room, low-density master planning, gated security, controlled traffic, and a residential demographic of senior executives and family buyers. Residents report measurably higher wellbeing scores, with daily access to green space and walking trails, particularly families with children and remote-working professionals.

Year-round green outlook
Reason 03

Capital Appreciation

Emirates Hills golf view villas have appreciated 60-85% since 2020, Dubai Hills Estate villas 45-65%, and Jumeirah Golf Estates townhouses 50-70%. Capital growth outpaces the broader Dubai residential market by 1.5-2.5x. The appreciation premium reflects both scarcity and rising international demand for golf community property from UK, European, and South Asian buyers post-pandemic.

+60-85% since 2020
Reason 04

Rental Premium

Golf view properties command 15-25% higher rents than equivalent non-golf properties in the same community. A 4-bedroom Jumeirah Golf Estates Earth Course townhouse rents at AED 380,000/year; the same townhouse on an interior plot rents at AED 290,000/year. Golf view also commands a premium on short-term holiday lets during the November-March peak season.

+15-25% rents
Dubai's Golf Courses

Six Championship Fairways

Dubai has built one of the world's most concentrated golf course ecosystems — nine 18-hole championship courses within a 30-minute drive of the city centre. Six of these anchor freehold residential communities and shape the surrounding property market. Each course has its own character, designer signature, and tournament history. Below is a summary of the six courses most relevant to golf view property buyers, with the communities and price brackets they anchor.

Course 01

Emirates Golf Club

Majlis & Faldo Courses

Est. 1988 · Dubai's oldest 18-hole course

Emirates Golf Club is the original anchor of Dubai golf, opened in 1988 with the Majlis Course designed by Karl Litten. The Majlis hosts the annual Dubai Desert Classic — the European Tour's longest-running Middle East event. The Faldo Course, added in 1996, offers floodlit night golf and a links-style challenge. The club sits within the Emirates Hills freehold community, where villas backing onto the Majlis fairway trade at AED 3,200-4,500 per sqft — the highest golf view premium in Dubai. Resident-tier membership is available to Emirates Hills and surrounding community residents at preferred rates.

Course 02

Dubai Hills Golf Club

Championship Course

Est. 2019 · Emaar Properties masterplan

Dubai Hills Golf Club opened in 2019 as the centrepiece of Emaar's Dubai Hills Estate masterplan, designed by European Golf Design in collaboration with Greg Norman's design team. The 71-par championship course runs 7,300 yards with the Burj Khalifa skyline visible from elevated fairways. Property surrounding the course has appreciated 45-65% since opening, with golf view villas in Park Heights and Sidra districts commanding the steepest premium. The club offers full practice facilities, a golf academy, and resident-tier membership rates for Dubai Hills Estate property owners.

Jumeirah Golf Estates

Earth & Fire Courses

Est. 2007 · Greg Norman signature

Jumeirah Golf Estates features two Greg Norman-designed championship courses — the Earth Course, host of the DP World Tour Championship season finale since 2009, and the Fire Course, a more forgiving layout for handicap play. The community offers the most accessible entry point for golf view ownership, with townhouses overlooking the Earth Course from AED 4M and villas from AED 8M. Freehold since 2007, the community has matured into one of Dubai's most established golf residential enclaves, with full retail, school, and club facilities on-site.

Arabian Ranches Golf Club

Desert-Style Course

Est. 2004 · Ian Baker-Finch design

Arabian Ranches Golf Club opened in 2004 as one of Dubai's first desert-style courses, designed by Ian Baker-Finn with a links feel and dramatic sand waste areas. The 18-hole, par-72 layout sits within the Arabian Ranches freehold community, with golf view villas in Alma, Mirador, and Saheel districts commanding a 15-20% premium over interior plots. The club offers full floodlit night golf, a Golf Digest academy, and resident-tier membership for Arabian Ranches property owners. Arabian Ranches remains one of Dubai's most family-friendly golf communities.

Golf Properties FAQ

Common Golf Property Questions

Eight questions ZARIN advisors hear most often from golf course property buyers. If yours isn't covered, message us on WhatsApp for a direct answer — typically within an hour during business hours.

What is the price premium for golf course properties in Dubai?
Golf course properties in Dubai typically trade at a 20-35% premium per square foot compared to equivalent non-golf properties in the same community. A 4-bedroom villa overlooking the Earth Course at Jumeirah Golf Estates sells at AED 1,800-2,400 per sqft, while the same villa on an interior plot trades at AED 1,400-1,800 per sqft. Emirates Hills golf-view villas command an even higher premium — AED 3,200-4,500 per sqft versus AED 2,400-3,000 for non-golf-view plots. The premium reflects the scarcity of fairway frontage, unobstructed green views, and the lifestyle cachet of golf community membership.

Which golf communities are best for property investment?
The strongest investment cases are Emirates Hills for capital preservation, Dubai Hills Estate for balanced growth and yield, and Jumeirah Golf Estates for entry-level golf view ownership. Emirates Hills has appreciated 60-85% since 2020 with very low turnover, reflecting its status as Dubai's most established gated golf enclave. Dubai Hills Estate has seen 45-65% appreciation over the same period thanks to the new Dubai Hills Mall and Downtown Dubai proximity. Jumeirah Golf Estates offers the most accessible entry point from AED 4M for a golf view townhouse, with Earth Course frontage the most sought-after. ZARIN's advisory models each community's 5-year total return including rental, capital growth, and exit liquidity.

Can non-golfers buy properties in golf communities?
Yes — most residents in Dubai's golf communities are not active golfers. The appeal extends well beyond the sport itself: golf communities offer manicured green outlooks, low-density master planning, strong security, controlled traffic, and a residential demographic of senior executives and family buyers. Property owners typically receive preferred golf club membership terms and discounted green fees, but membership is never mandatory. Many buyers cite the unobstructed fairway view and the absence of neighbouring towers as the primary draw, with golf itself a secondary benefit. ZARIN advisors help non-golfers evaluate the lifestyle, community fees, and view corridors of each golf community on its own merits.

Are golf course properties a good investment in Dubai?
Golf course properties have been among Dubai's strongest-performing residential sub-segments since 2020. Capital appreciation of 50-85% has been recorded across Emirates Hills, Jumeirah Golf Estates, and Dubai Hills Estate, driven by scarcity of fairway frontage and strong international demand from golf-playing buyers in the UK, Europe, and South Asia. Rental yields run 4-5% gross for villas and 5-6% for townhouses, with golf view adding 15-25% to achievable rent. The investment case rests on the finite supply of golf community plots — Dubai has 9 championship courses and almost no new golf masterplans in advanced development. ZARIN provides a 5-year total return model for every golf view mandate.

What are the best golf clubs in Dubai?
Dubai's six championship golf clubs each have distinct character. Emirates Golf Club hosts the annual Dubai Desert Classic on its Majlis Course, with the Faldo Course offering a floodlit 18-hole alternative. Dubai Hills Golf Club is a 71-par championship course designed by European Golf Design, set against the Burj Khalifa skyline. Jumeirah Golf Estates features two Greg Norman-designed courses — Earth (host of the DP World Tour Championship) and Fire. Arabian Ranches Golf Club offers an 18-hole desert-style course designed by Ian Baker-Finch. The Els Club at Dubai Sports City was designed by Ernie Els, and the Address Montgomerie is a Colin Montgomerie signature course. ZARIN advisors can arrange trial rounds and membership introductions.

Do golf community properties come with golf club membership?
Not automatically. Property ownership in a golf community does not include golf club membership, but most clubs offer preferred membership categories and discounted joining fees for residents. At Jumeirah Golf Estates, property owners receive a 30-40% discount on the standard AED 35,000-65,000 annual membership. Emirates Golf Club offers resident-tier memberships for residents of surrounding communities. The Els Club at Dubai Sports City and Address Montgomerie offer similar resident rates. Membership is always optional — many property owners never play golf. ZARIN provides a full breakdown of membership categories, fees, and residency-based discounts for each golf community before purchase.

What is the average price of a villa in Emirates Hills?
Emirates Hills villa prices range from AED 15 million for a 4-bedroom original-condition villa on a smaller interior plot to AED 200+ million for a golf view mansion on Sector E or Sector M. The median transaction in 2024-2025 was approximately AED 28 million for a 6-bedroom villa. Golf-view plots (those backing onto the Emirates Golf Club Majlis Course) command AED 3,200-4,500 per sqft, while interior plots trade at AED 2,400-3,000 per sqft. Plot sizes range from 8,000 sqft to over 60,000 sqft, so absolute prices vary widely. Emirates Hills is Dubai's most established gated golf enclave and the lowest-turnover trophy community in the city — typical holding periods exceed 12 years.

Can foreigners buy freehold properties in Dubai golf communities?
Yes — all five major golf communities (Emirates Hills, Dubai Hills Estate, Jumeirah Golf Estates, Arabian Ranches, and Tilal Al Ghaf) are designated freehold areas where foreign nationals of any country can buy outright freehold title in their own name. There are no nationality restrictions and no requirement for a UAE residency visa. Title deeds are issued by Dubai Land Department and the property can be inherited, sold, or mortgaged like any other freehold asset. Emirates Hills and Jumeirah Golf Estates have been freehold since their original launches in 2003 and 2007 respectively, while Dubai Hills Estate and Tilal Al Ghaf are newer freehold masterplans. ZARIN handles the full transfer process including KYC, NOC, and DLD registration.
