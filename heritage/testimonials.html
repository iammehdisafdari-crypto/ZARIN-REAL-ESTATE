The Short Version

This page collects twelve testimonials from ZARIN Real Estate clients — buyers, borrowers, off-plan purchasers, a seller and after-sales clients. Every quote was shared with the client’s permission; names are abbreviated and transaction details anonymised, consistent with the discretion we practise across the Private Office. You will find no star ratings embedded in this page’s code: self-serving review markup breaches Google’s own guidelines, so the testimonials here are visible cards — readable by people, not harvested as a schema score. What the quotes describe is the six-stage advisory process run by one advisor end to end, with costs disclosed upfront: a 2% buyer-paid commission, the 4% DLD transfer fee and an AED 4,580 admin fee — AED 124,580 in total on an AED 2,000,000 cash purchase. The founder has invested in Dubai real estate since 2005; ZARIN Real Estate, established in 2023, runs client mandates with the same discipline. For the same journeys told with full numbers, read the case studies.

Reading This Page

How to Read These Testimonials

Four rules govern every quote on this page. They exist because the clients behind them are buying and selling significant assets in a market where discretion has commercial value — the privacy-first position our Private Office is built on. The rules are applied identically to praise and to criticism.

  1. 1
    Shared with permission

    Every quote on this page was offered by a client in connection with a closed mandate — nothing was incentivised, and no client was paid or discounted for contributing. Where a client preferred a phrase changed, it was changed; where they asked for removal, it was removed.

  2. 2
    Names abbreviated

    Attribution follows one convention: an initial and a city — A. T., Tehran; K., Bengaluru; J., London. Enough to signal the buyer profile a journey describes, not enough to identify anyone. No full names appear anywhere on this page.

  3. 3
    Transactions anonymised

    Prices are rounded, addresses are generalised to the district, and dates are loosened to the season. The detail that matters — the budget ceiling, the yield figure, the fee arithmetic — survives; the detail that identifies does not.

  4. 4
    Edited for length, never meaning

    Quotes are trimmed for space and lightly copyedited for grammar. Meaning is never altered: where a client criticises us, the criticism stays — which is why section eight exists.

Why no star ratings on this page

We publish no rating scores and embed no review schema: marking up reviews of your own business in your own code is self-serving and breaches Google’s guidelines for review rich results. The testimonials stand as visible, quotable text — judged by what they say, not by a number we would be awarding ourselves.

Journey 1 of 4

Buying Journeys

Three purchases, three entry points: a first-time buyer clearing the Golden Visa threshold, a returning buyer with a hard ceiling, and a relocation on a clock. The common thread is a shortlist that respected the brief and an offer anchored on recorded comparables. The same journeys with full arithmetic sit in the case studies; the process itself is documented on the buying advisory page.

“It was my first property purchase outside Iran and I expected to be handed between departments. Instead one advisor ran everything — shortlist, viewings, negotiation, transfer. The Business Bay apartment closed at AED 2M and the Golden Visa file opened the week after. Every fee was on paper before I committed a dirham.”

A. T., Tehran
Buying · First Dubai Purchase
Business Bay · AED 2.0M · Golden Visa

“We set a hard ceiling of AED 2.2M and braced for the upsell. It never came — the shortlist stayed inside the budget, and each option arrived with its yield maths attached. The Dubai Marina unit we chose shows 6.4% gross, and that figure came from the data, not from the brochure.”

R. & S., Mumbai
Buying · Second Purchase
Dubai Marina · Within an AED 2.2M budget

“I was relocating from London with a six-week window and no patience for portal roulette. Three viewings, all three of them right, and an offer anchored on DLD-recorded comparables rather than asking prices. The transfer completed inside my window, and nobody asked me to negotiate against a listing price with a straight face.”

H., London
Buying · Relocation Purchase
Downtown Dubai · Comparables-led negotiation
Journey 2 of 4

Financing & Visa Journeys

Money crossing borders is where Dubai purchases go wrong — remittance rules, mortgage coordination and visa timing each have their own failure mode. These three journeys ran through the India and UK desks and the Golden Visa route with structure settled before property. Mortgage finance is placed with partner banks and coordinated by the advisor; visa thresholds are confirmed in writing before commitment, not discovered after.

“The India Desk structured my remittance to be FEMA-compliant before a single dirham moved, then coordinated a 60% LTV mortgage with partner banks. I assumed the cross-border part would be the ordeal. It was the most orderly part of the purchase.”

K., Bengaluru
Financing · First Dubai Mortgage
60% LTV · Partner banks · FEMA-compliant remittance

“As a non-dom I needed the ownership structure settled before the purchase, not patched together after it. The UK Desk worked the tax structuring through with my own advisers and kept the purchase timeline honest. Nothing in that file was improvised.”

J., London
Financing · Tax Structuring
UK Desk · Non-dom structuring

“The visa was the point, not the property. The AED 2M threshold, the 10-year term and the family sponsorship were confirmed in writing before we chose the apartment. We bought in order to qualify; we did not buy and then hope.”

N. & F., Tehran
Visa · Family Golden Visa
AED 2M threshold · 10-year term · Family sponsorship

The Balanced Part

What Clients Push Back On

A testimonials page with only praise is an advertisement. These are the three pushbacks we hear most often — published in the clients’ own framing, with our response underneath each. We keep them on the page because a balanced record is a more useful decision tool than a flattering one: the same standard the awards page applies to recognition.

What clients tell us

Verification takes longer than portal browsing

“I wanted five viewings on Saturday. ZARIN wanted budget, financing and intent verified first — it took a week longer than portal browsing and I chafed at it. It also meant the shortlist was three flats, not thirty, and all three were right.”

— E., Dubai

Our take: Guilty. Understand Mandate comes before Unbiased Filtering and the sequence is not negotiable — a shorter, correct shortlist is the product, not a delay to it.

What clients tell us

ZARIN declines projects it will not sell

“I asked for a listing at a price the comparables did not support. They declined the listing. I was annoyed for a month; I understood the discipline by the following quarter.”

— T., Abu Dhabi

Our take: Also guilty. A mandate we cannot execute honestly is a mandate we return — a decline is a service, not a slight.

What clients tell us

Advisors push back on over-budget briefs

“My brief said AED 3M in Downtown; my bank balance said AED 2.2M. The advisor refused to shop the edge of affordability and made me revise the brief instead. The purchase that followed was dull — which is exactly what a good purchase should be.”

— G., Mumbai

Our take: We would rather lose the brief than run a client into the edge of affordability. The budget wins; the wishlist negotiates.

Testimonials FAQ

Eight Questions, Answered Honestly

How these testimonials are sourced, anonymised and balanced — answered without adjectives. If your question is not here, ask us directly.

Are these reviews verified?
We claim no verification badge, and we deliberately publish no star-rating markup: embedding self-serving review schema on your own page breaches Google’s guidelines for review rich results. What we can vouch for is provenance — every quote comes from a client of record, was shared with that client’s permission, and traces internally to a closed mandate. If that is not enough for you, ask the next question.

Can I speak to a past client before engaging?
Sometimes, yes. Introductions are arranged case-by-case and only with both parties’ consent — the client being contacted has as much say in the matter as you do. Where a suitable match exists and both sides agree, we make the introduction; where it does not, we say so rather than manufacture one. Clients who prefer silence are exactly the clients whose testimonials carry an abbreviated name.

Why are names abbreviated and transactions anonymised?
Because privacy is the default at ZARIN, not an upgrade. Our clients are buying and selling significant assets in a market where discretion has commercial value — the same positioning our Private Office is built on. We would rather publish twelve honest, anonymised voices than twelve identifiable ones and a weaker promise of confidentiality.

Do you publish only positive testimonials?
No. Section eight carries the three criticisms we hear most often — that verification is slower than portal browsing, that we decline projects we will not sell, and that our advisors push back on over-budget briefs. A testimonials page without friction is an advertisement; this one is a record. Every quote here survived because the client stands behind it, not because it flatters us.

Who will actually handle my purchase?
One senior advisor, end to end. Mandates run the six-stage process — Understand Mandate, Unbiased Filtering, Due Diligence, Negotiate & Structure, Conveyancing & Closing, Stay Supported — with a single advisor owning your file throughout; the India, UK and China desks join when a mandate needs them and never replace the owner. The team is small and senior by design — meet it on the team page.

What does ZARIN charge a buyer?
A 2% buyer-paid commission, the DLD transfer fee of 4%, and an admin fee of AED 4,580 — all disclosed before you commit. On an AED 2,000,000 cash purchase that totals AED 124,580, or 6.2%; with a 60% LTV mortgage, AED 132,830, or 6.6%. The full arithmetic, and the process behind it, sits on the buying advisory page.

I am buying from abroad and cannot view properties. Can you still advise?
Yes — remote mandates are a standard part of our book, including purchases executed entirely under power of attorney. What does not change is the diligence: verified data, inspected fact and documented process substitute for your attendance, never for the checks themselves. The fifth case on the case studies page shows exactly how such a file runs.

How long has ZARIN been operating?
The founder has invested in Dubai real estate since 2005 — two decades that include two project-level investments, the 2008 crisis and RERA certification. ZARIN Real Estate, established 2023, is the brokerage built on that experience: a small senior team operating the India, UK and China desks, working in English, Arabic and Farsi. The founder’s letter tells the sequence properly.
