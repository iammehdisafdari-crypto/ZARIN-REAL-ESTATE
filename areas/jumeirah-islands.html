Masterplan & Vision

Fifty Islands,
One Unified Water Sanctuary

Crafted by master developer Nakheel, Jumeirah Islands is one of the world's most distinctive residential engineering achievements. Rather than conventional grid layouts, the master development comprises 50 circular islands surrounded by vast interconnected artificial lakes, creating an oasis of serenity in the heart of modern Dubai.

Of the 50 islands, 46 are residential villa clusters containing exactly 16 luxury villas each, preserving intimate neighborhood enclaves. Two additional clusters accommodate townhouses, while dedicated central islands host the vibrant Jumeirah Islands Clubhouse and the convenience-driven Jumeirah Islands Pavilion retail center.

Six Architectural Themes & Modern Adaptations

The community was designed with six architectural expressions: European, Islamic, Mediterranean, Oasis, Tropical, and Contemporary. Over the past five years, high-net-worth investors and family offices have driven an aggressive modernization trend, upgrading classic facades into floor-to-ceiling glass modernist estates with landscaped infinity pools overlooking the water.

The Waterfront Advantage: Scarcity & Generous Plots

Unlike newer developments characterized by high density and narrow lot lines, Jumeirah Islands villas sit on substantial private plots ranging from 10,000 to over 14,000 square feet. This generous space allows owners to extend living areas, integrate outdoor entertainment pavilions, and enjoy expansive private frontage to calm, aerated lakes.

& Renovation Arbitrage

With only 736 villas across the entire master development, Jumeirah Islands offers an irreplaceable combination of mature greenery, extensive water frontage, and land-value appreciation.

Fixed Villa Supply

Permanent Scarcity of 736 Homes

Unlike peripheral suburban master developments that can continually expand phases, Jumeirah Islands has been completely built out since 2008. The strictly limited supply of 736 villas ensures long-term capital preservation and pricing power.

Eight Questions, Answered Properly

Essential facts, ownership details, and market insights for prospective buyers in Jumeirah Islands.

What is the current entry price for a villa in Jumeirah Islands? ↓
Practical entry for a standalone 4-bedroom villa in original Nakheel condition starts around AED 15M to 16M. Standard 5-bedroom villas trade between AED 18M and 22M, while high-spec fully upgraded and extended turnkey modern estates command between AED 25M and 36M+. Jumeirah Islands 4-bedroom townhouses on the perimeter offer entry from approximately AED 8.5M.
Who developed Jumeirah Islands and when was it built? ↓
Jumeirah Islands was master-developed by Nakheel Properties. Launched in 2003 and handed over between 2006 and 2008, the community pioneered Dubai's luxury inland water-living concept with 50 man-made circular islands set amidst extensive artificial lakes.
How do the different architectural clusters compare? ↓
The 46 residential villa clusters are organized into six distinct architectural themes: European, Islamic, Mediterranean, Oasis, Tropical, and Contemporary. While interior floor plans (such as Entertainment Foyer and Garden Hall) share core structural foundations, their external rooflines, window styles, and colonnades vary. Today, many owners transform their facades into sleek modern contemporary white villas.
What are the rules and potential for renovating or extending a villa in Jumeirah Islands? ↓
Nakheel and Dubai Municipality permit extensive internal and external remodeling, including plot extensions, adding built-up area (such as basement or rooftop terraces), modern glass curtain walls, and enlarged swimming pools. Turnkey architectural renovations often generate an arbitrage margin of 25% to 40% above total acquisition and construction costs upon resale.
What are the annual service charges in Jumeirah Islands? ↓
Community service charges in Jumeirah Islands are among the most attractive for prime Dubai villa districts, averaging approximately AED 3.80 to AED 4.50 per sqft of built-up area annually. For a standard 5,300 sqft villa, this amounts to roughly AED 20,000 to AED 24,000 per year, covering 24/7 manned security gates, common lake aeration and maintenance, landscaping, and road infrastructure.
Can foreign nationals purchase freehold property in Jumeirah Islands? ↓
Yes, Jumeirah Islands is 100% designated freehold. Foreign buyers of any nationality can purchase, lease, mortgage, and hold full title deeds issued directly by the Dubai Land Department (DLD). Purchases above AED 2M also qualify the owner and their family for the UAE 10-year Golden Visa.
How does Jumeirah Islands compare to Jumeirah Park and The Meadows? ↓
Jumeirah Islands sits as the tier-one luxury community among its neighbors. The Meadows offers family-oriented Emaar suburban living without gated island clusters. Jumeirah Park offers newer villas but lacks natural water features. Jumeirah Islands provides private 16-villa island clusters, direct lake frontages for over 75% of properties, larger plot sizes (10,000-14,000 sqft), and an exclusive boutique clubhouse.
What lifestyle amenities are available at the Jumeirah Islands Clubhouse? ↓
Operated in partnership with Sunset Hospitality, the Jumeirah Islands Clubhouse features the renowned Isola Ristorante Italiano, The Pool day club with cabanas, Joe's Backyard gastropub, Raise Fitness & Wellness gym, and salon facilities, creating a private resort hub for residents.
