The Short Version

Twenty-four episodes since the January 2026 launch, running thirty to fifty minutes each. The format is simple: developer interviews that ask about delivery records rather than launch parties, area deep-dives across the eight districts ZARIN tracks, buyer stories told with the mistakes left in, and tax structuring for international investors — the India, UK and China desks each take an episode. Every number quoted on air comes from DLD records or the RERA index, the same sources as our market reports. Our founder has invested in this market since 2005; the firm established in 2023 records the show with the same discipline it advises with.

About the Show

The Market, Spoken Aloud

The ZARIN podcast is the audio arm of the insights desk: the same DLD-sourced numbers, the same named desks, the same refusal of paid placements — recorded instead of typeset. It exists for the parts of the market that survive conversation better than text: a developer explaining a delay, an advisor walking a buyer’s actual numbers, a desk lead describing how remittances really clear.

It is built for three listeners: the buyer mid-decision who wants the area deep-dive on their shortlist, the international investor structuring a purchase from abroad, and the existing client who would rather hear the quarterly breakdown on the drive home than read forty-two pages. No episode assumes prior Dubai knowledge; every episode assumes you can handle a number.

The show is free on all platforms, with no premium tier and no advertorials — consistent with the editorial standards across the blog and the market reports.

Three Formats, One Standard

Episode Formats

The show runs three standing formats, each with a data segment attached: the relevant DLD numbers read out, dated, and left with the listener — because a claim you cannot check is a slogan, not analysis.

Developer Interviews

Developers on their own projects — asked about delivery records, escrow discipline and handover quality, not launch-party adjectives. The hardest question is always the one about the last delay.

Formats: roughly every third episode

Area Deep-Dives

One district per episode, on the locked eight-area table — price per sqft, gross yield, service-charge load and the supply pipeline. The audio companion to the area pages.

All eight core districts covered

Buyer Stories

Real purchases told with permission and anonymised — including what went wrong. Episode 20 walks three journeys end to end, mistakes included, because that is where the lessons live.

Told in the “M., Tehran” convention
Every format closes with the numbers

Each episode ends with a short data segment: the current quarter’s figures for whatever the episode touched, sourced from DLD records and the RERA index, and repeated in the show notes on each platform. Q3 2026’s baselines: AED 1,650/sqft citywide, +12% year on year, 38,247 transactions.

How We Record

Prepped on the Record, Not the Press Release

Every episode is prepared from primary sources. Before a developer interview, the desk pulls the delivery record and the escrow history. Before an area deep-dive, the current DLD price and volume series for that district — the same series behind the market data pages. Before a quarterly breakdown, the corresponding market report is the script.

The guest hears the hard questions in advance — that is a courtesy, not a concession — but never edits them. Where a guest declines to answer on the record, the silence stays in the episode. It is usually the most informative part.

And the rule that governs everything: no pay-to-play. Developers do not pay for coverage, and they cannot pay for ratings. The scorecards discussed on air are the same ones published on the Developer Reputation Tracker — built the same way our founder has judged counterparties since 2005, and the firm established in 2023 made public.

The Recording Rules
Sources
DLD transaction records and the RERA service-charge index — dated on air.
Questions

No pay-to-play, for coverage or for ratings, without exception.
Corrections

Podcast FAQ

Eight Questions Listeners Ask First

Platforms, cadence, guests and the no-pay-to-play rule — answered before you hit play. Anything else, write to the desk.

Where can I listen to the ZARIN podcast?
The show is carried on Spotify, Apple Podcasts and YouTube. We deliberately publish no direct streaming links on this page — apps and regional catalogues differ, and a stale link is worse than none. Search “ZARIN Real Estate” in your podcast app and the show appears with the gold Z mark. Every episode is free to stream or download.

How often are new episodes released?
The show launched in January 2026 and has published two to three episodes a month since — 24 episodes through October 2026, with the run continuing through Q4. Area deep-dives and quarterly report breakdowns anchor the calendar, with desk episodes and buyer stories between them. Subscribers to the monthly briefing receive an alert the morning each episode goes live.

Do developers pay to be featured?
No — and they cannot pay for ratings either. There is no pay-to-play on the show: developers do not pay for coverage, for scorecard positions, or for the softening of a hard question. This is the same standard the Developer Reputation Tracker is built on, and it is why we can say what a delivery record actually looks like.

Who appears on the show?
ZARIN advisors host, with the founder joining for the quarterly breakdowns and market episodes. Guests sit across the market: developers on their own projects, and the country desks in person — Rohit Kapoor on India, Emma Whitfield on the UK, Li Hong on China. Buyer stories are told with permission and anonymised, in the “M., Tehran” convention used across the site.

Can I suggest a topic or ask a question on air?
Yes — and listeners do. Write to advisory@zarinrealestate.com with “Podcast” in the subject, or use the contact page. Recurring listener questions become desk episodes: the Golden Visa myths episode began as three separate emails asking the same thing.

Are the episodes free to listen to?
All 24 episodes are free, on every platform, with no premium tier and no advertorials. The show exists for the same reason the blog and the market reports do: informed buyers make better clients, and better clients make a better advisory business.

Where do the numbers in the episodes come from?
The same sources as everything ZARIN publishes: Dubai Land Department transaction records for prices and volumes, the RERA service-charge index for costs, and the ZARIN ROI basis for yields. Each quarterly breakdown episode is recorded against the corresponding market report, and the live figures sit on the market data pages.

I'm a first-time buyer — where should I start?
Start with Episode 20, “Buyer Stories: Three Purchases, Three Lessons” — it walks three real journeys, including the mistakes. Then take the area deep-dives for whichever districts are on your shortlist, and read the buyer’s guides alongside. The show assumes no prior Dubai knowledge; neither do the guides.

Prefer reading? The blog library covers the same ground in 200+ articles.
