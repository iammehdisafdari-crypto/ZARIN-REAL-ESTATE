Our Promise

Three Non-Negotiable Principles

01

Knowledge Before Selling

We invest in understanding before we earn the right to advise. Every mandate begins with a deep market briefing — area analysis, developer due diligence, yield projections, and exit strategy modeling — so that every recommendation is grounded in institutional-grade research, not commission incentives.

02

Transparency Before Promising

We disclose what others omit. Service charges, transfer fees, developer escrow status, handover delay risk, net versus gross yield, and cross-border tax exposure — all documented before you sign. Support Until Completion

Our mandate does not end at handover. Title deed registration, utility activation, property management setup, tenant sourcing, annual yield optimization, and disposition strategy — we remain your fiduciary long after the keys are handed over. This is what we mean by custody, not just closing.

Our Process

Six Stages of Measured Acquisition

Every ZARIN mandate follows the same institutional process — from mandate definition through long-term custody.

  1. 01

    Understand Mandate

    We begin with a comprehensive mandate definition — investment thesis, risk parameters, lifestyle requirements, exit strategy, and timeline. No property is shown until we understand the buyer.

  2. 02

    Unbiased Filtering

    From the full Dubai market inventory, we filter to a shortlist of 5-8 properties that match your mandate — including off-market opportunities not available through public portals.

  3. 03

    Due Diligence & Audit

    Independent valuation, developer reputation check, escrow health verification, service charge analysis, title deed verification, and legal review — documented in a formal due diligence report.

  4. 04

    Negotiate & Structure

    Price negotiation, payment plan structuring, SPA review, and deal architecture — optimized for your tax position, currency exposure, and financing strategy.

  5. 05

    Conveyancing & Closing

    DLD transfer, NOC acquisition, title deed registration, utility activation, and key handover — managed end-to-end by ZARIN's in-house legal and conveyancing team.

  6. 06

    Stay Supported (Custody)

    Property management, tenant sourcing, annual yield optimization, portfolio rebalancing, and disposition strategy — your fiduciary partner long after handover.

Frequently Asked

Questions About Dubai Real Estate

Can foreigners buy freehold property in Dubai?

Yes. Since the 2002 Freehold Decree, foreigners can own freehold property in designated Dubai areas including Palm Jumeirah, Downtown, and Dubai Marina. Title deeds are issued by the Dubai Land Department (DLD). Read our non-resident buyer guide →

What is the minimum investment for a Golden Visa in Dubai?

The 10-year Golden Visa requires a property investment of AED 2 million (USD 545,000) minimum, verified by the Dubai Land Department and GDRFA. Check your eligibility →

Are off-plan properties safe to invest in Dubai?

Yes, when regulated by RERA. The Escrow Law (Law No. 8 of 2007) requires buyer funds in RERA-monitored escrow accounts, released only at verified construction milestones. Read investment guide →

What are the additional costs when buying property in Dubai?

Budget for: 4% DLD transfer fee, 2% agency commission, AED 4,000 title deed fee, 2% mortgage registration (if financed), and annual service charges of AED 10-30 per sqft. Use cost calculator →

Can I get a mortgage as a non-resident in Dubai?

Yes. Non-residents can borrow up to 50-65% LTV from UAE banks (Emirates NBD, Mashreq, ADCB) at 3.5-5.5% variable rates linked to EIBOR. Speak to mortgage advisor →

What is the average ROI on Dubai real estate?

Dubai gross rental yields range 5-9%. Prime areas (Palm Jumeirah, Marina) yield 5-6%; emerging areas (JVC, Business Bay) reach 7-9%. Net yields average 1.5-2% lower. Calculate your ROI →

How does the property buying process work in Dubai?

The process: 1) Price agreement, 2) MOU signed with 10% deposit, 3) Developer NOC, 4) DLD title transfer (4% fee), 5) Key handover. Off-plan buyers sign SPA and pay per payment plan. Read buyer's guide →

Can I pay for property with cryptocurrency in Dubai?

Yes. Several Dubai developers accept BTC, ETH, and USDT for property purchases above AED 5 million, subject to KYC/AML compliance through regulated exchange partners. Learn about crypto payments →
