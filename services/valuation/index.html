Market Price Assessment —
What Your Dubai Property Is Worth Today

A DLD-anchored market appraisal — comparables, rent, yield and a written pricing band within 48 hours. Free, and free of obligation.

The Short Version

A ZARIN Market Price Assessment is anchored to DLD transaction comparables, not to optimism. It is free, and it carries no obligation — the same methodology and the same report whether you sell with us, list elsewhere, or simply want the number. The written report ships with five named deliverables: a selling price recommendation, a rental price assessment, a current market competition analysis, a property selling strategy and — where relevant — an off-plan resale read. Three approaches are weighted by property type: comparable transactions, the income approach, and replacement cost as a sanity-check for villas. What You Receive

Five Deliverables, One Free Assessment

Every written Market Price Assessment ships with five named outputs — the price, the rent, the competition, the strategy and, where relevant, an off-plan resale read. Each is a decision document in its own right; together they are what free actually buys. The same five arrive whether you sell with us, list elsewhere, or simply want the number.

01

Selling Price Recommendation

A recommended market-entry price, not just an estimate of worth. We derive an asking band with a defensible floor from DLD recorded comparables, adjusted for your unit’s floor, view, and condition — so you list at a price the current buyer pool will actually engage with, rather than a number that quietly excludes you from search results.

02

Rental Price Assessment

For owners considering letting instead of (or while) selling: an achievable rental range — furnished and unfurnished — grounded in lettings evidence for your building and area, with the gross yield each scenario implies and the trade-offs of single versus multi-cheque terms made explicit.

03

Current Market Competition Analysis

A head-to-head read of the units you are actually competing against: comparable apartments and villas actively listed for sale and for rent right now — their asking prices, time on market, and condition differences — and where your property wins or loses against each of them.

04

Property Selling Strategy

The recommended entry price paired with a route to market — private network, agent-to-agent, or controlled public launch — and a pre-agreed review cadence: if screened viewings and offers are absent after three to four weeks on correctly priced stock, we say when and by how much to revise (typically in 3-5% steps), and what feedback justifies holding instead.

05

Off-Plan Resale Assessment

For owners who bought off-plan and want to exit before handover: an assignment-focused read of your position — premium or discount to the developer’s current list price, Oqood transfer mechanics, developer NOC and assignment-fee requirements, the depth of the pre-handover buyer pool, and the timing windows around construction milestones that move resale value.

Inside the Written Report

Six evidence components feed the five deliverables above — the same raw material a paying client would receive, printed in the report rather than summarised away:

  • DLD comparables — same building & area: recorded transactions, not asking prices — adjusted for floor, view, condition and tenure before they are read against your unit.
  • Three-year price trend: where the building and the area have moved since your purchase — momentum, not just a level.
  • Active listing competition: what a buyer can buy instead of your property today, and at what asking prices.
  • Achievable rent & gross yield: what the unit lets for against the area benchmark, and the gross yield that implies.
  • Service-charge & net adjustment: service charges netted out of the headline so the reported figures reconcile to what you actually keep.
  • Suggested pricing band: a defensible range with a strategy note — what the band assumes, and what moves you within it.
Methodology

How We Value — Three Approaches

The same three lenses an institutional investor would apply, weighted by what the asset actually is. The founder has priced Dubai assets this way since 2005 — long before the firm was established in 2023.

01

Comparable Transactions

Primary for apartments and villas alike. We read recorded DLD transactions — same building where they exist, area level where they do not — and adjust each for floor, view, condition and tenure. The weight of evidence sits with what has actually transferred, not what is being advertised.

02

Income Approach

For yield assets, value is checked from the income side: achievable rent capitalised at the area’s gross yield. The worked example below runs a Dubai Marina apartment end to end — gross rent, service charges, net income, net yield — the same arithmetic that appears in your report.

03

Replacement Cost

A sanity-check used mainly for villas and unique builds: land value at the area’s prevailing rate plus current construction cost, depreciated for age and condition. It rarely sets the number, but it catches comparables that have drifted.

The weighting changes with the asset

Apartments lean on comparables; villas blend comparables with replacement cost; yield stock — Business Bay and Marina studios, for instance — is checked hard against the income approach. Penthouses and unique builds get all three, plus a candid note on the limits of each. The weightings and the arithmetic are printed in the report, not buried.

Adjustments

What Moves Your Property’s Value

The instant widget applies five of these. The written report weighs all eight against your unit’s specifics — and shows the arithmetic.

01

Floor & View

High floors with unobstructed sea or skyline views trade at a premium — the canonical full-view adjustment runs +8-12%. Low floors facing the community trade at the base rate.

02

Condition & Refurbishment

A renovated unit commands roughly +5% against original condition; a unit needing work gives roughly the same back. Refurbishment that repays itself at sale is scoped, not assumed.

03

Service Charges vs Area

High service charges suppress value twice — in net yield and in buyer appetite. AED 16/sqft in a Marina tower is normal; AED 30 in the same tower is a story the comparables will tell.

04

Tenure & Title

Freehold has been open to all nationalities in designated zones since 2002 — but encumbrances, mortgagor notings and unresolved service-charge debts all sit on title, and all discount.

05

Tenant Status

Vacant possession widens the buyer pool; a tenanted sale narrows it — and an Ejari rent below market can discount the price by more than the rent gap itself.

06

Building Age & Maintenance

A disciplined owners’ association and a funded maintenance regime hold value; a deferred-maintenance building leaks it — usually at sale, when the buyer’s surveyor arrives.

07

Micro-Location

Within an area, position is price: road frontage versus quiet plot, metro walk versus drive, the tower’s better side. Area benchmarks are the starting line, not the answer.

08

Market Depth

Buildings with thin transaction volume price on the last trade; buildings with volume price on the market. Thin stock means wider pricing bands and slower sales.

Pre-Handover Exits

Off-Plan Resale Assessment

This assessment is for owners who bought off-plan and now want to exit before handover — the payment plan is part-paid, the unit is months or years from completion, and the question is what the contract is worth to someone else today. Before completion you are not selling a title; you are assigning a contract. That single fact changes the pricing logic end to end, which is why off-plan resale earns its own deliverable in the written report.

The mechanics run through assignment. With the contract Oqood-registered and before title issuance, you sell by assigning the Sale & Purchase Agreement to a new buyer. The developer’s NOC is required for every assignment — fees commonly run AED 500-5,000, but some developers charge a percentage of the price instead; the figure is developer-specific, and we disclose the exact requirement before you commit to anything. An assignment administration fee may apply on top, and DLD charges its standard 4% on the assignment transfer.

Pricing is where off-plan resale earns its own analysis. Pre-handover stock typically trades at a discount to the developer’s current list price for the same unit type — early buyers captured the launch price, while late buyers pay the developer’s marked-up price but get a shorter wait and visible construction in return. Your position sits somewhere on that curve, so we benchmark it against both the developer’s live pricing and recent recorded assignments in the project. What we will not do is present the developer’s list price as your resale value.

Timing moves the number. The value milestones are payment-plan progress, construction percentage and handover proximity — each one narrows the risk a buyer is paying you to carry. And the buyer pool is structurally narrower: mortgage availability on pre-handover stock is limited, so assignments sell mostly to cash or high-down-payment buyers. The depth of that pool at your milestone is a number we count, not a feeling.

What we deliver is the Off-Plan Resale Assessment from the five deliverables above: your premium or discount to the developer’s live price, the Oqood and NOC mechanics, the assignment-fee position, the depth of the pre-handover buyer pool, and the timing windows around construction milestones. If you are weighing a new off-plan purchase rather than an exit, the off-plan investment guide covers the buying side; when the exit is ready to execute, our selling advisory runs it.

Know Which Number You Are Holding

Market Appraisal, DLD Indexed Valuation, Bank Valuation — Three Different Numbers

Three numbers can attach to the same Dubai property in the same month — legitimately. A market appraisal prices a decision; the DLD’s indexed figure computes the transfer fee; a bank’s panel valuation sets the lending limit. Knowing which one you are holding, and what it is for, is half the analysis.

The Use Cases

When You Need an Assessment

Seven recurring situations — each with a slightly different question. A seller asks what the market will pay; a landlord asks what it will let for; a bank asks what it can safely lend against; an estate asks what the asset was worth on a given date. The methodology flexes; the evidence base does not.

01

Preparing to Sell

The pricing band and the strategy note are the difference between listing at market and wishing. A free assessment for any serious seller — the mandate to sell with ZARIN is a separate choice, made later, in writing.

02

Selling or Letting?

The two deliverables that decide it sit side by side in the report: the selling price recommendation against the rental price assessment and its gross yield. We model both against your service charges and the void risk between tenancies — then the asset’s next decade, not this month’s headlines, picks the route.

03

Talking to Your Bank About Refinancing

Non-resident terms run at 50-65% loan-to-value on EIBOR-linked rates — but the figure that matters at the bank is their own panel valuation, not ours. We explain what the bank’s valuation will do, where it is likely to land against market, and what that means for the equity you can release.

04

Insurance Renewal

Building cover should track replacement cost, not purchase price. The assessment sets the reinstatement basis so you are neither under-insured at claim time nor paying to over-insure — and the renewal negotiation starts from evidence, not from last year’s premium.

05

Probate & Inheritance Planning

Estates need defensible values for DIFC Wills and cross-border succession. Formal purposes need official routes — we point you to them — and the assessment prepares the figures and organises the file so those conversations start properly. Complex estates escalate to our Private Office.

06

Annual Portfolio Mark-to-Market

A portfolio managed without annual values is managed blind. The yearly review marks each asset to market, benchmarks it against its area, and rebalances against the mandate.

07

Golden Visa Evidence

Property at or above AED 2M qualifies for the 10-year Golden Visa — and the application needs evidence, not a hunch. The visa process relies on DLD-recorded value, not our assessment; we prepare the figures the Golden Visa filing requires and flag where the recorded value and the market appraisal diverge.

Assessment FAQ

Eight Questions Owners Ask First

Cost, accuracy, data, timelines and what the report can be used for — answered with method rather than adjectives. If your question is not here, ask us directly.

Is the market price assessment really free?
Yes — free means free. There is no fee, no obligation to list, sell or transact with ZARIN, and no charge if you take the report and walk away. We publish assessments for three reasons: some recipients later become selling clients; some become buyers; and some simply tell other people what we are like to deal with. All three have worked since the founder began investing in 2005, and the firm was established in 2023 on the same arithmetic. What there is not is a small print that converts a free assessment into a mandatory listing agreement. If you later ask us to sell, that mandate is a separate document you choose to sign.

Is this an official valuation?
No. A ZARIN Market Price Assessment is an advisory market appraisal — an indicative valuation prepared for pricing decisions, not a formal valuation. Banks require their own panel valuation before they lend, and the Dubai Land Department’s indexed valuation governs transfer fees; neither can be replaced by our report. Tell us what you need the number for and we will say plainly which official valuation that purpose requires — the assessment prepares you for the conversation, it does not substitute for the document.

How accurate is it?
The assessment is anchored to recorded DLD comparables and delivered as a band, not a single number — a point estimate on Dubai property is false precision when the same unit can legitimately clear a 5-10% band depending on exposure, condition and the buyer’s circumstances. We are explicit about the honest error bars, and about what would move your unit outside them: a refurbishment, a tenancy change, a view obstruction, or a shift in the building’s listing competition. The instant-estimate widget on this page is deliberately wider still — treat its range as orientation. Accuracy, properly defined, is whether the market lands inside the band we publish — and the annual mark-to-market of custody files keeps us honest about that.

What data do you use?
DLD recorded transactions first — same building where they exist, area level where the building is thin — adjusted for floor, view, condition and tenure. Around that core: the live listing competition for sale and for rent, lettings evidence for your building and area, service-charge ledgers netted out of headline figures, and the three-year price trend for the building and area. Portal asking prices appear only as competition context, never as evidence of value. The canonical area benchmarks are the same ones that drive our ROI tools, so the numbers reconcile across everything we publish.

Do you assess off-plan properties?
Yes — as the Off-Plan Resale Assessment, the fifth deliverable where it is relevant. Before completion you are not selling a title; you are assigning a contract, so the assessment works through assignment dynamics, the developer’s live pricing, Oqood registration, the developer NOC and assignment-fee position, the depth of the pre-handover buyer pool, and the timing windows around construction milestones. The off-plan resale section sets out the mechanics. If you are buying off-plan rather than selling, our off-plan investment guide covers the due diligence properly.

What if I disagree with the recommendation?
Then we would like to hear the argument, because a good disagreement usually improves the report. The evidence is shown line by line — every comparable, every adjustment, every assumption — so you can challenge the inputs rather than the output. You set the final price; it is your property. What the strategy note does is set out the consequences of pricing above the band honestly: what it costs in time-on-market and eventual negotiation, and what feedback would justify holding instead.

Do you need to visit the property?
Not for the indicative level — the instant estimate and the first read of the assessment work from the title or contract details, floor plans, your photographs and the DLD comparable record. The written assessment benefits from a visit or a video walkthrough, and we will recommend one when it changes the number materially: unique villas, refurbished units where finishes matter, or buildings where the recorded comparables have gone stale. Remote is fine — ZARIN completes these assessments for overseas owners routinely, and you are never asked to fly in to be assessed.

Can I use it for mortgage, probate, or visa purposes?
No. Those routes need official valuations: a bank-appointed valuation for lending, and the DLD’s own processes for transfer fees and indexed value — the Golden Visa, for instance, relies on DLD-recorded value, not on our assessment. What the assessment does is prepare you for those conversations: it flags where a conservative bank number is likely to land below market, and it organises the evidence file. It prepares you for the official routes — it does not replace them.
