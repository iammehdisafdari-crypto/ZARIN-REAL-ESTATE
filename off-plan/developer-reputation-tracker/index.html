How ZARIN Calculates Reputation

Every developer on this tracker carries a composite score on a 0-100 scale, built from four weighted indicators: on-time delivery (30%), construction quality (25%), complaint volume per 100 units (20%), and financial stability (25%). Inputs are pulled from Dubai Land Department transaction records, RERA project registrations and complaint filings, owner-submitted snagging reports (1,400+ entries across 38 developers), and sentiment analysis of 14 active owner communities. We deliberately weight on-time delivery highest because it is the most directly observable buyer cost — rent bridge, opportunity cost on capital, and disrupted life plans. We do not accept paid placements or developer-supplied scores; every input is external and verifiable. Scores recalculate on the first business day of each month; major regulatory actions trigger an out-of-cycle review within 24 hours, with corrections published transparently on this page. Full data sources, scoring weights, and update cadence are detailed in the methodology section below.

The four cards on the right display the scoring weights applied to each indicator. The exact same weights apply to every developer on the tracker — we do not adjust weights per developer or per marketing relationship. This provides objective, empirical comparability across developers of different scales and asset classes.

Methodology · Part 1

Four Risk Indicators Explained

Every ZARIN developer score is built from the same four indicators, weighted to reflect what actually matters to off-plan buyers. Below we explain each indicator — what it measures, how it is calculated, and what thresholds trigger green, amber, or red badges.

30% weight

On-Time Delivery

Measures whether projects are delivered by the contractual handover date specified in the Sales Purchase Agreement. This is the single most actionable metric for off-plan buyers — it directly impacts cash flow and residency planning.

Calculated from DLD Oqood-to-Title-Deed conversion timestamps against the original SPA handover date. Average delay figures are in calendar months. Green <2.0mo, Amber 2.0-3.0mo, Red >3.0mo.

25% weight

Quality Rating

Letter-grade assessment of construction quality based on post-handover snagging reports, owner surveys, and building inspection audits. Captures architectural finishes, MEP reliability, and warranty responsiveness.

Aggregated from 1,400+ owner-submitted snagging reports across 38 active developers, normalised by unit count. A+ requires <0.8 snags per unit and >90% defect-resolution rate within warranty.

20% weight

Complaint Volume

Normalised count of formal complaints per 100 delivered units, sourced from RERA complaint filings, consumer protection authority records, and verified owner community forums.

Normalised by delivered units to avoid scale bias. A complaint index below 1.0 reflects market-leading customer satisfaction. 1.0-1.5 is standard. Above 2.0 indicates systemic disputes.

25% weight

Financial Stability

Composite assessment of escrow account discipline, project funding ratios, corporate debt leverage, and where available, published audited financials.

We monitor RERA escrow account compliance ratios (buyer funds held in regulated accounts vs released based on engineering milestones) and historical cancellation track records.

Badge Threshold Guidance

Green (Low Risk) — Meets or exceeds market-leading standards. Amber (Medium) — Acceptable industry median, warrants specific unit review. Red (High) — Elevated risk, requires thorough contract and escrow due diligence prior to booking.

Methodology · Part 2

Data Sources, Scoring & Transparency

ZARIN's Developer Reputation Tracker is built on six structured data streams. The Dubai Land Department (DLD) provides Oqood-to-Title-Deed conversion timestamps, which give us day-level precision on handover timing — the foundation of the on-time delivery metric. RERA provides project registrations, escrow account compliance status, and the formal complaint registry, which underpins both the complaint index and the financial stability indicator. For qualitative signals we monitor news archives from 2014 onward, run sentiment analysis on 14 active owner-community forums, and index owner-submitted snagging reports — currently 1,400+ verified entries across 38 developers.

Scoring weights were calibrated by reviewing post-purchase dispute records and 200+ private investor consultations: on-time delivery at 30%, construction quality at 25%, complaint volume at 20%, and financial stability at 25%.

Update cadence is layered. Active project status refreshes daily via RERA's registry. Complaint and sentiment signals refresh weekly. The full composite score recalculates on the first business day of each month. Major regulatory events trigger immediate manual audits within 24 hours.

Tracker FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

The eight most common questions investors ask about how our tracker operates, what its scores signify, and how to apply them in real-world acquisition decisions.

How does ZARIN track developer reputation?
We aggregate six structured data streams — DLD transaction records, RERA project registrations, RERA complaint filings, news archives spanning 2014-2026, social media sentiment analysis, and owner-submitted snagging reports — into a weighted composite score. The methodology has been refined over 18 months of internal use and is reviewed quarterly by our advisory team. Each developer is scored on four indicators: on-time delivery (30%), construction quality (25%), complaint volume (20%), and financial stability (25%). The composite score is then ranked, with the top 10 published on this page.

What data sources does the tracker use?
The primary structured source is the Dubai Land Department's transaction and Oqood-to-Title-Deed conversion records, which give us handover timing with day-level precision. RERA provides project registration data, escrow account compliance status, and formal complaint filings. For qualitative signals we use news archives (English, Arabic, and Farsi press), property forum sentiment analysis across 14 active owner communities, and a self-submitted snagging report dataset of 1,400+ entries from verified buyers.

How often is the tracker updated?
Active project status (handover dates, phase openings) is refreshed daily via RERA's project registry API. Complaint and sentiment signals update weekly. The full composite score is recalculated on the first business day of each month, and historical trend charts are rebuilt at the same cadence.

Can I trust smaller developers with high scores?
A high score from a smaller developer should be read differently from a high score at scale. Sobha Realty, Select Group, and Ellington all post strong metrics — but their sample sizes are smaller, and a single late project can swing their numbers more than it would for Emaar or Nakheel. We flag this by including 'completed projects' count in the leaderboard; below 30 completed projects, treat the score as indicative rather than definitive.

What is a good on-time delivery percentage?
Above 85% on-time delivery (within 30 days of the SPA handover date) is the threshold we consider market-leading. Emaar, Sobha, Meraas, and Ellington all sit above this line. Between 80% and 85% is acceptable but indicates recurring scheduling pressure — typical of large-volume developers like Nakheel or DAMAC. Below 80% warrants caution.

How does ZARIN verify complaint data?
Complaint data comes from three verified sources: RERA's formal complaint registry, UAE Consumer Protection Agency filings, and our own curated dataset of owner-community forum reports. We normalise all complaint counts by delivered units to avoid penalising larger developers for raw volume; what matters is the rate per 100 units.

Does the tracker cover all Dubai developers?
We currently track 38 active Dubai developers, covering approximately 96% of all off-plan units sold in the past 24 months. The 10 displayed on the leaderboard are the highest-scoring; the remaining 28 are available on request through your ZARIN advisor.

Can I request a developer not listed?
Yes. Use the 'Speak to Analyst' button to request a custom report on any Dubai-licensed developer, including those not yet in our published tracker. We will compile what data is available from public RERA and DLD records and provide an objective due diligence summary.
