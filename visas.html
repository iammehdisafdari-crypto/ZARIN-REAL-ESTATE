Visa Overview

Dubai offers four visa types through property investment: Golden Visa (10-year, AED 2M+), Investor Visa (2-year, AED 750K+), Retirement Visa (5-year, age 55+), and Remote Work Visa (1-year). Each covers different buyer profiles — from long-term investors to digital nomads. ZARIN manages the full application with GDRFA and Dubai Land Department.

The 10-Year Residency Pathway

The Golden Visa is the UAE's flagship residency program for property investors. A property investment of AED 2 million or more grants 10-year residency for the investor, spouse, and children under 18. No minimum stay requirement — just visit the UAE once every 6 months.

Frequently Asked

Visa Questions

What is the Dubai Golden Visa?

The Golden Visa is a 10-year UAE residency visa granted to property investors who purchase property worth AED 2 million or more. It covers the investor, spouse, and children under 18. View Golden Visa guide →

What is the minimum property investment for a Golden Visa?

The minimum property investment for a 10-year Golden Visa is AED 2 million (approximately USD 545,000). The property must be verified by the Dubai Land Department and GDRFA. Check eligibility →

Can I get a Dubai visa by buying property?

Yes. Dubai offers several visa types through property investment: Golden Visa (10-year, AED 2M+), Investor Visa (2-year, AED 750K+), and Retirement Visa (5-year, age 55+ with AED 2M+ property). Compare visa types →

How long does the Golden Visa application take?

The Golden Visa application typically takes 2-4 weeks after property purchase and title deed transfer. ZARIN's visa advisory team manages the full process with GDRFA. View process →

Does the Golden Visa cover my family?

Yes. The Golden Visa covers the investor, spouse, and children under 18. Children over 18 can get a dependent visa if they are unmarried and studying. View family coverage →

What is the difference between Golden Visa and Investor Visa?

The Golden Visa is a 10-year residency requiring AED 2M+ property investment. The Investor Visa is a 2-year residency requiring AED 750K+ property investment. Golden Visa offers longer duration and more benefits. Compare →

Can Indian citizens get a Golden Visa through Dubai property?

Yes. Indian citizens are eligible for the Golden Visa through Dubai property investment of AED 2 million or more. ZARIN's India Desk assists with FEMA compliance and Rupee-to-AED payment pathways. View India guide →

Do I need to live in Dubai to keep my Golden Visa?

No. The Golden Visa does not require you to live in Dubai full-time. You must visit the UAE at least once every 6 months to maintain the visa. There is no minimum stay requirement. View requirements →
