Escrow & Due Diligence

Six Safeguards for Off-Plan Buyers

Every ZARIN-recommended off-plan project passes these six checks before we present it to a buyer.

01

Escrow Account Verified

Developer has a RERA-registered escrow account. Buyer funds are deposited into this account, not the developer's general account.

02

Developer Track Record

Developer has completed at least 3 projects in the past 5 years with 90%+ on-time handover rate.

03

Construction Permit

Project has received Dubai Municipality construction permit before any marketing or sales activity.

04

Project RERA Registration

Project is registered with RERA and has a valid project number that can be verified on the Dubai REST app.

05

Title Deed on Land

Developer holds title deed for the project land. Without this, the project cannot legally sell units.

06

Service Charge Analysis

ZARIN verifies projected service charges are reasonable and aligned with comparable ready properties in the area.

Frequently Asked

Off-Plan Investment Questions

Is off-plan property in Dubai a good investment?

Yes. Dubai off-plan properties in 2026 are priced 15-22% below replacement cost. Combined with structured payment plans, this creates built-in equity before handover. Read investment guide →

How does the escrow system protect off-plan buyers?

The Escrow Law (Law No. 8 of 2007) requires developers to deposit buyer funds into RERA-monitored escrow accounts. Funds are released only at verified construction milestones. Read buyer's guide →

What are the most common payment plans?

Common plans are 60/40 (60% during construction, 40% on handover), 70/30, and post-handover plans where 20-30% is paid after key handover over 2-3 years. Calculate your plan →

What is the expected ROI on Dubai off-plan properties?

Expected ROI ranges 6-8.5% gross yield. Prime areas yield 5-6%, emerging areas reach 7-9%. Off-plan typically offers 15-22% capital appreciation at handover. Predict your ROI →

How long does off-plan construction take?

Construction typically takes 24-36 months. ZARIN-vetted developers have a 96% on-time handover rate. Payment plans are structured around construction milestones. View developer tracker →

Can I sell my off-plan property before handover?

Yes. You can sell before handover if the developer allows assignment (usually after 30-40% of payment plan is paid). This is called flipping and can generate capital gains. Learn about selling →

What is the difference between off-plan and ready property?

Off-plan is bought from developers before construction completes, with payment plans and lower entry prices. Ready properties are completed and available immediately but cost 15-22% more. View ready properties →

Which Dubai developers are most reliable?

Top-rated developers include Emaar (98% on-time), Sobha (95%), Meraas (93%), DAMAC (92%), Nakheel (90%), and Binghatti (89%). ZARIN's Developer Reputation Tracker monitors each. View full tracker →
