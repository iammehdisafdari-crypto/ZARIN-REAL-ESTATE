Heritage · In His Own Words

The Letter, In Full

Written by the ZARIN Founder, dated Dubai, November 2026. Published unedited — the same letter handed to clients at their first consultation.

Dubai, November 2026

Most ‘about us’ pages are written by people who joined the company last quarter, about a founder they have met twice. I would rather you heard it from me. This letter is the honest version: who I am, what two decades of my own capital in Dubai taught me, and why I built a firm around it.

I entered this market in 2005 as an investor, not a broker. I was not looking for a job; I was looking after my own money. Early on I went further than most buyers ever do: I invested in two construction projects at the project level — the capital sat inside the development itself, not in a single finished apartment. From that seat you learn things no listing portal teaches: how a project is actually structured, how launch pricing is phased, where budgets quietly hide their risk, and which promises a developer can keep.

Then 2008 came. The global financial crisis hit Dubai harder than almost anywhere, and I rode it down with real capital at risk. It taught me the lessons only a drawdown can: paper gains are not wealth until they are liquid; leverage is a promise you make about a future you do not control; and in a crisis, escrow discipline and genuine end-users separate the projects that survive from the ones that vanish. My positions were bruised, not broken. The difference was discipline, not luck.

The 2014 freehold expansion opened the market wider, and I went deeper into off-plan and project-level work. That period taught me to read regulation as an investor: as the rules matured, value stopped accruing to whoever queued first at a launch and started accruing to whoever understood the masterplan, the service charges and the exit. It is a habit of analysis I have kept ever since.

In 2020 the world stopped, and Dubai paused with it. I watched the market do what it has always done once the shock passed: remember why it exists — transparent title, no annual income tax on property, and a city that keeps adding residents. The pandemic taught me patience as a strategy. The buying windows that follow panics reward the prepared and punish the rushed, and being ready usually matters more than being clever.

The boom of 2024 to 2026 has been the steepest of my career. Citywide pricing passed AED 1,650 per square foot by the third quarter of 2026, up roughly 12% year on year, with more than 38,000 transactions recorded in the quarter. A boom teaches the hardest lesson of all: restraint. When everyone is buying, discipline is the only edge left — verifying recorded comparables, reading the service-charge ledger, walking away from stretched launches. That is precisely when an investor’s counsel is worth the most, and a salesperson’s is worth the least.

I founded ZARIN Real Estate in 2023 because I could no longer watch good people get bad advice. Friends, family and fellow investors — many of them Iranian — kept asking me the same questions, and kept receiving answers from people who had never risked a dirham of their own. So I built the firm I wished had existed when I started: investor-led, deliberately small, advising in English, Arabic and Farsi, with a special duty to Iranian buyers who need someone fluent in both markets.

My promise to you is simple. You will hear what I would do with my own money — including when the answer is ‘not this one’. Every cost is disclosed before you sign: our 2% buyer-paid commission, the DLD transfer fee of 4%, AED 4,580 of admin, and roughly thirty days from agreement to transfer. One named advisor carries your file from the first conversation to the title deed, and stays after it. No hidden costs, no invented urgency.

If you have read this far, the short version: I have been an investor in this city since 2005, and I built ZARIN so you could borrow that experience before spending your own money. That is the whole business. Everything else on this website is detail.

— The Founder, ZARIN Real Estate

Investor in Dubai real estate since 2005. Founder & Principal, ZARIN Real Estate, established 2023.

Why ZARIN Exists

An investor, not a salesperson

ZARIN was founded in 2023 to sell that experience, not inventory. When the answer is ‘not this one’, we say it, because a salesperson who says that earns nothing and an advisor who says it earns trust.

Built around the Iranian buyer

Iranian investors face questions others do not — capital transfer routes, dual-market fluency, sanctions-aware banking practicalities, and advisors who actually speak Farsi. The founder built the Iran desk first and personally, because it was the desk he wished had existed for his own community. Every document and conversation runs in Farsi, Arabic or English.

Contracts explained in Farsi, Ejari and owners’ association matters handled in Arabic, negotiation in whichever language the deal thinks in — Dubai’s paperwork genuinely runs in all three, and so do we. One advisory standard regardless of which of them you speak.

Founder’s Letter FAQ

Eight Questions About the Man Behind the Letter

Why no name, whether he still invests, and who actually runs your file — answered plainly. If your question is not here, ask us directly.

Why doesn't the founder use his name publicly?
It is deliberate. This firm sells experience, not personality. Keeping the founder’s name off the marketing keeps the focus where it belongs — on the advice, the numbers and the process — and it protects the discretion that many of our Iranian and regional clients rightly expect from a private advisory. The founder is not hiding: he signs every letter like this one, meets clients at engagement, and leads the firm’s investment judgement every working day.

Is the founder still an active investor?
Yes. He has been active in Dubai real estate since 2005 and continues to hold and review his own positions — two construction investments at the project level among them. That matters to clients for one reason: the man advising you is exposed to the same market you are about to enter, on the same fundamentals we publish in our market reports. Skin in the game is not a slogan here; it is the business model.

Can I meet the founder?
Yes — on substance, not ceremony. The founder attends first consultations where the mandate warrants it: complex portfolios, off-plan project questions, or files where his project-level experience changes the answer. On routine purchases the senior advisor who leads your file is the better conversation, because they will be the one running your six stages end-to-end. Ask for either; you will get a straight answer about which one you need.

Who leads my file if I engage ZARIN?
One named senior advisor, from the first conversation to the title deed and beyond. ZARIN is deliberately a small senior team — no junior handoffs, no call-centre rotation. The founder set that structure when the firm was founded in 2023, because institutional memory is the service: the person who negotiated your purchase is the person who handles your tenancy query in month fourteen. Meet the people on our team page.

What exactly did the founder invest in at the project level?
Two construction projects in Dubai, with capital committed inside the developments themselves rather than in finished units. The specifics — locations, counterparties, returns — stay confidential, for the same discretion reasons we extend to every client file. What we can share is what it taught him: how developers structure budgets, phase pricing and manage escrow, which is precisely the knowledge we apply when assessing off-plan projects for buyers.

Why did the founder wait until 2023 to start ZARIN?
Because until then he was an investor, not an advisor — and the two disciplines are different. Two decades of cycles since 2005, including the 2008 crisis and the 2024–2026 boom, convinced him that the advice ordinary buyers were receiving was getting worse even as the market got better. He founded ZARIN in 2023 to build the investor-led alternative, sized deliberately small so the standard could not dilute.

Does the founder's own investing create conflicts of interest?
It creates alignment, and we manage the edge cases in writing. The founder never buys, pre-reserves or takes positions in a project a client is actively evaluating, and ZARIN takes no developer incentives — our income is the 2% buyer-paid commission, disclosed before you sign. Where his holdings could overlap a client brief, the file is flagged and handled by another senior advisor. Skin in the game without conflicts is the entire design.

How do the founder's lessons change how ZARIN advises buyers today?
Directly. The 2008 lesson means we stress-test leverage and verify escrow before anything else. The 2014 lesson means we read masterplans and service charges, not launch brochures. The 2020 lesson means we plan holding periods around fundamentals rather than sentiment. And the 2024–2026 boom means we practise restraint — comparing every asking price against DLD-recorded comparables in our market data before a client commits a dirham.
