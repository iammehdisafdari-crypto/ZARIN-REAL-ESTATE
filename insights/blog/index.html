How We Publish

Editorial Standards

Four rules govern every article that carries the ZARIN name. They exist because the blog's job is to be useful to a buyer mid-decision — and a buyer mid-decision needs sources, not adjectives.

Data-First, Always

Every price traces to DLD transaction records; every cost figure to the RERA service-charge index; every yield to the ZARIN ROI basis. Numbers are dated to their quarter — Q3 2026 baselines: AED 1,650/sqft citywide, +12% year on year, 38,247 transactions.

Named Desks, Real Accountability

Articles are signed by the desk that wrote them — ZARIN Editorial, ZARIN Research or the ZARIN Visa Team — and the people behind each desk are profiled openly.

Meet the authors →

No Paid Placements

No developer funds an article, a ranking or a “review”. We publish entry prices, service charges and yields — including the unflattering ones — because credibility is the only asset a publisher really owns.

Corrections & AI Disclosure

Material corrections are logged on the page, never silently edited. Where AI-assisted research supports a draft, the drafting process is disclosed under our AI policy — the numbers are always ours to verify.

Blog FAQ

Eight Questions Readers Ask First

Authorship, sourcing, permissions and where to begin — answered the way the articles are written, with specifics. If your question is not here, ask us directly.

Is the ZARIN blog free to read?
Every article is free — no paywall, no gated “premium” content, and no newsletter required to read a full piece. The same data-first standard applies across the whole library: if we publish a number, we can show you where it came from. The monthly briefing simply delivers new articles to your inbox; it is a convenience, never a toll gate.

Who writes ZARIN's articles?
Three named desks author the library: ZARIN Editorial for buyer-facing guides, ZARIN Research for market and area analysis, and the ZARIN Visa Team for residency pathways. Every byline is a real desk with accountability — the people behind each one are profiled on our authors page. Anything touching pricing or yield methodology is reviewed by the founder before publication.

How often do you publish new articles?
New pieces appear continuously through the month across the three desks, with heavier clusters around each quarterly report cycle. Rather than checking back, most readers take the monthly intelligence briefing, which carries every new article alongside the report summary and podcast alerts. There is no spam and unsubscribing takes one click.

Are the articles just disguised sales pitches?
No. The editorial standard is explicit: no paid placements, no developer-funded “reviews”, and no article written to steer you toward a specific listing. We publish entry prices, service charges and yield figures — including the unflattering ones — because the blog exists to earn trust before a mandate begins, not to close one. Where an article relates to a ZARIN service it is linked openly, and the analysis stands on its own.

Can I quote or republish ZARIN articles?
Yes, with attribution. Journalists, researchers and fellow advisers regularly cite our data; we ask that any quotation names ZARIN Real Estate and links to the original article. For republication of full pieces, or use of our tables in commercial materials, write to advisory@zarinrealestate.com first and we will normally say yes.

What data sources stand behind the articles?
Dubai Land Department transaction records anchor every price and volume figure; the RERA service-charge index anchors the cost data; and our own advisory files — anonymised — supply the process detail. The market data pages carry the current baselines: a citywide average of AED 1,650/sqft, up 12% year on year, and 38,247 transactions in Q3 2026. Where an article uses an assumption rather than a record, it says so.

Where should a first-time buyer start?
Start with the six buyer’s guides — they walk the full journey from mandate to landlord in order. Then read Off-Plan vs Ready Property before any launch viewing, because that single decision shapes payment terms, timelines and risk. The Start Here section on this page maps the common entry points.

Do you cover visas and ownership costs as well as property?
Yes — the visa desk writes the residency pathways, the AED 2M Golden Visa chief among them, and the editorial desk maintains the cost guides, including the full transaction stack of the 4% DLD transfer fee, 2% buyer-paid commission and AED 4,580 admin fee. Service charges by district are covered too, because a purchase that looks thin on price alone can look worse once the annual bills arrive. The visa eligibility checker turns the reading into an answer.
