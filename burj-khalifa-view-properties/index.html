Five Towers, One Iconic Sightline

Burj Khalifa view property is concentrated across five clusters in central Dubai — Downtown Dubai, the Opera District, Business Bay, Burj Vista, and The Address Boulevard. Each offers a different angle on the Burj: full-frame from across the Boulevard, side-profile from Business Bay, or fountain-and-Burj from the Address line. The choice shapes both lifestyle and capital value, because a fountain-side Burj view in The Address Boulevard trades 8-15% higher than a Burj-only view in Burj Vista. ZARIN advisors carry per-floor view diagrams for every tower and can identify which specific units genuinely see the Burj — and which are marketed as Burj view but face the rear courtyard instead.

Why Burj View

The Case for the Burj Sightline

A Burj Khalifa view is the most concentrated piece of address equity in Dubai residential. Only 35-45% of Downtown apartments actually see the Burj, and Dubai's view-corridor regulations prevent new towers from blocking existing sightlines — which means the supply of Burj view units is effectively fixed while demand keeps rising. Below are the four reasons ZARIN recommends Burj view mandates to clients seeking both lifestyle and capital preservation in central Dubai.

Reason 01

Iconic Landmark

The Burj Khalifa is the world's tallest building and Dubai's single most recognised global icon. Owning a home that looks directly at it — particularly during the evening fountain shows and New Year fireworks — is a permanent lifestyle and status statement that no other Dubai view can replicate. The view itself becomes a primary memory of the home.

830m of iconography
Reason 02

Premium Pricing

Burj view units trade at a 25-40% premium per sqft over equivalent rear-facing units in the same building. A 3-bedroom in Burj Vista with full Burj view sells at AED 2,800-3,400 per sqft versus AED 1,900-2,300 for the same line on the opposite side. Fountain-and-Burj dual views in The Address Boulevard command an additional 8-12% over Burj-only views.

+25-40% price premium
Reason 03

Rental Demand

Burj view apartments — particularly 1- and 2-bedroom units in Downtown towers — are the most requested short-term lettings in Dubai. During peak season (November to March) DTCM-licensed Burj view units run 75-85% occupancy and command 30-50% nightly premiums over comparable inland stock. Long-term rents are also 20-35% higher than rear-facing equivalents.

+30-50% peak rents
Reason 04

Capital Appreciation

Downtown Dubai overall has appreciated 55-75% since 2020, with front-facing Burj view units outperforming rear-facing equivalents by 12-18 percentage points. Burj Vista and The Address Boulevard have been among the top-five capital appreciation towers in the city. The view-corridor protection makes future supply effectively fixed — appreciation is structurally supported.

+55-75% since 2020

Burj View FAQ

Common Burj View Questions

Eight questions ZARIN advisors hear most often from Burj view buyers. If yours isn't covered, message us on WhatsApp for a direct answer — typically within an hour during business hours.

What is the price premium for Burj Khalifa view properties in Dubai?
Burj Khalifa view properties in Dubai trade at a 25-40% premium per square foot compared to equivalent units with city, road, or rear-facing orientations in the same building. A 3-bedroom apartment in Burj Vista with full Burj view typically commands AED 2,800-3,400 per sqft, while the same line on the opposite side trades at AED 1,900-2,300 per sqft. The premium is highest for front-facing units on high floors (40+) with unobstructed sightlines, and narrows for side-angle views or partially obstructed vistas. Fountain-side apartments in The Address Boulevard command an additional 8-12% over Burj-only views because they capture the Dubai Fountain shows and Burj Khalifa New Year fireworks from the same window. ZARIN advisors track per-floor price curves and help clients identify where the premium is genuinely justified versus where the asking price is inflated.

Which buildings have the best Burj Khalifa views?
The strongest Burj Khalifa sightlines come from five clusters. Burj Vista Towers 1 and 2 sit directly opposite the Burj on Mohammed bin Rashid Boulevard and offer the closest full-frame view in Dubai — many units are within 250 metres of the tower. The Address Boulevard and Address Sky View provide fountain-and-Burj dual views from high floors. Opera District buildings (Fortune Perla, IL Primo, and Grand Bleu Tower) offer an angled Burj view across the Opera Plaza with less crowding. Belhoul Gardens and Burj Khalifa itself offer the iconic at-the-base perspective. Business Bay towers on the Al Khail Road side (Executive Towers, Blue Bay Tower, and select DAMAC projects) provide a side-profile Burj view at a more accessible price. ZARIN maintains a per-building view-corridor map of every Downtown and Business Bay tower, including which floors clear neighbouring obstructions.

Are Burj view properties a good investment?
Burj Khalifa view properties have been among the strongest-performing sub-segments of Dubai residential since 2020. Downtown Dubai overall has appreciated 55-75% over the past five years, with front-facing Burj view units consistently outperforming rear-facing equivalents by 12-18 percentage points. Rental demand is robust — Burj view 1- and 2-bedroom apartments are the most requested short-term lettings in Dubai during peak season (November to March), and Airbnb-style operators report 75-85% occupancy for premium Burj view stock. Capital appreciation is supported by finite supply: very few new towers with clear Burj sightlines are being delivered, and existing sightlines are protected by Dubai's view-corridor regulations. ZARIN's advisory includes a 5-year total-return model comparing Burj view, beachfront, and inland alternatives for every mandate.

What is the difference between fountain view and Burj view?
A Burj view apartment looks directly at the Burj Khalifa tower itself, typically from a Downtown or Business Bay tower with the Burj in the sightline. A fountain view apartment overlooks the Dubai Fountain and Burj Lake, with the Burj Khalifa usually visible behind or alongside the fountain — but the primary visual anchor is the water show rather than the tower. Fountain view apartments in The Address Boulevard and Burj Vista command a 8-15% premium over Burj-only views because they capture the evening fountain shows (every 30 minutes after sunset) and the New Year fireworks. Some units offer both fountain and Burj views simultaneously — these are the most sought-after and trade at the highest price per sqft. ZARIN advisors carry per-floor view diagrams that distinguish full-Burj, partial-Burj, fountain-only, and side-angle units in every Downtown building.

Can I rent out a Burj view property short-term?
Yes — short-term letting (daily and weekly) of Burj view apartments is permitted across Downtown Dubai, Business Bay, and the Opera District, provided the building is registered with Dubai's Department of Tourism and Commerce Marketing (DTCM) as a holiday home licensee. Most major Downtown towers including Burj Vista, The Address Boulevard, and Burj Khalifa itself are DTCM-registered, but specific unit eligibility depends on the owners' association and building management policy. Short-term rentals of Burj view 1- and 2-bedroom apartments during peak season (November to March) typically yield 6-8% gross annually, versus 4-5% for long-term rentals of the same unit. ZARIN's property management arm operates short-term lettings on behalf of owners and handles DTCM licensing, guest vetting, and 24/7 concierge — typically taking 20% of gross rent.

What are the service charges for Burj view buildings?
Service charges for Burj view buildings in Downtown Dubai and the Opera District typically run AED 22-32 per sqft annually, higher than the Dubai average of AED 16-22 for inland towers. Burj Vista towers charge around AED 24-28 per sqft, The Address Boulevard around AED 30-34 per sqft (reflecting hotel-grade amenities and concierge), and Burj Khalifa itself AED 95-110 per sqft (the highest in Dubai, reflecting the iconic address and Armani Hotel services). Business Bay towers with side Burj views are lighter — typically AED 16-22 per sqft. Service charges cover district cooling (which is mandatory in Downtown and accounts for 35-45% of the bill), common-area maintenance, lift systems, security, and sinking fund. ZARIN provides a full three-year service-charge audit and sinking-fund projection before any offer is submitted.

Do all apartments in Downtown Dubai have Burj views?
No — only around 35-45% of apartments in Downtown Dubai have direct Burj Khalifa sightlines. The masterplan has buildings on all four sides of the Burj, and units facing away from the tower (towards Financial Centre Road, Al Asayel Street, or Sheikh Zayed Road) see the city skyline or Business Bay towers instead. Even within a Burj-facing building, only units on the Burj side have the view — the opposite side of the same corridor typically faces the rear courtyard or neighbouring towers. Lower floors in some buildings may have Burj views partially obstructed by adjacent towers; high floors almost always command an unobstructed sightline. ZARIN advisors carry floor-by-floor view diagrams for every Downtown tower and can identify which specific units on which floors genuinely see the Burj before a viewing is booked.

How much does a Burj view penthouse cost in Dubai?
Burj view penthouses in Dubai range from AED 15M for an entry-level 3-bedroom penthouse with partial Burj view in Business Bay, to AED 120M+ for a full-floor Sky Penthouse in Burj Khalifa itself or The Address Sky View. A typical 4-5 bedroom Burj-facing penthouse in Burj Vista or The Address Boulevard trades at AED 22-45M, while ultra-premium Sky Penthouses in Address Sky View and Burj Khalifa start at AED 35M and reach AED 80-120M for full-floor Sky Collection units. Per-sqft pricing for Burj view penthouses typically runs AED 4,000-5,500, versus AED 2,800-3,400 for lower-floor Burj view apartments in the same building. ZARIN's Private Office maintains a discreet register of off-market Burj view penthouse owners willing to sell to qualified buyers.
